# Forge Source — Matthael's First Dungeon

**Status:** FORGE / SOURCE MATERIAL
**Canon role:** Sided out from Q31–Q37 answer set; extract only durable information separately.

## Source

- The story is titled "Jak Matthael vypálil svůj první dungeon".
- It follows Matthael after his escape from Dragon Barrow and after Naevyra was taken from him by Vaust.
- Matthael enters a dungeon hidden beneath a low, overgrown hill.
- Matthael discovers Riftblade inside the dungeon.
- Frostfire is depicted as new and somewhat uncontrolled at this point.
- Matthael's contact with Riftblade triggers a vision / trans-like state.
- A dragon Conscience awakens in response to Matthael touching Riftblade.
- Silverstrain reacts around Matthael during the awakening.
- Riftblade communicates through vibration / feeling rather than ordinary speech.
- Matthael experiences a strong sense of compatibility with Riftblade.
- The source depicts Matthael's early voice as irreverent and comedy-heavy.
- Matthael burns through much of the dungeon while experimenting with his power.
- Matthael leaves carrying Riftblade and a grotesque golden statue of a juggling duck.
