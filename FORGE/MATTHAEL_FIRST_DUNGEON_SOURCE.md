# How Matthael Burned His First Dungeon

**Status:** FORGE / source material

This is retained as literary source material rather than wholesale canon.

Useful continuity-bearing elements extracted from the story:

- Matthael encounters a dungeon after escaping with Naevyra from Dragon Barrow.
- Matthael's Frostfire is still new and somewhat uncontrolled during this period.
- Matthael's first dungeon is accidentally set on fire through his use of Frostfire.
- Matthael discovers Riftblade inside the dungeon.
- Riftblade's awakening is presented through white flame, silver smoke, runes, Frostfire imagery, and a nonverbal sense of recognition.
- The story frames the encounter as Matthael's first major contact with his sword's deeper significance.
- The story ends with Matthael leaving the burning dungeon carrying Riftblade and an absurd golden duck statuette.

The story itself does not resolve the creator of Riftblade, its arrival at the dungeon, or the future meaning of Crownbreaker.
