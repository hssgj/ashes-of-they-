13. Optional Ashes Mechanic - Impossible Logic
Impossible Logic
Starting at level 7, once per long rest, Barten may attempt an action that should normally be impossible, 
as long as it is explained through arcane engineering, battlefield geometry, device manipulation, chain￾system analysis, Silverstrain interference, mathematical prediction, or relay overclocking.
Situation DC
Hard but normal device problem 17
Dangerous magical mechanism 18
Active enemy relay/anchor 20
Boss mechanic interference 21
Chaincaller-style forced control 23
Silverstrain / reality fracture manipulation 24
Finale-level impossible action 25+
 Check options: INT Arcana, INT Investigation, INT Tinker’s Tools, INT Thieves’ Tools, INT Mason’s Tools,
or INT Smith’s Tools.
 Failure should almost never mean nothing happens. Better failures: Barten takes force/psychic damage,
a relay shuts down, one infusion flickers, an enemy marks him, Silverstrain backlash scars his hands, 
or the effect works but creates a later complication.
 Design rule: “This is stupid, fatal, and mathematically correct.” Not: “I can do anything because I am 
smart.”

15. Book I Finale Special Rules - Barten Player Version
These rules are for the final Chaincaller fight if Barten is played by a player. The point is not to punish the 
player; the point is to give Barten the most heroic possible win condition.
Last Deed Protocol
 Trigger when at least two are true: Chaincaller attempts to reset the battlefield; one or more Crew 
members are restrained/claimed; the final damage window is closing; escape route is collapsing; 
Barten has identified the missing variable; PLAYER is unable to physically stop him without ruining the
solution; the Crew cannot win by damage alone.
 Activation check: INT Investigation, INT Arcana, or INT Tinker’s Tools.
 Base DC: 25. Reduce DC by 1 for each relevant preparation: studied Chaincaller device, Red Glass Relay 
fragment, Dragon Barrow containment notes, Silverstrain data, Crew help, preserved Relay Node, 
successful Impossible Logic earlier in the fight. Minimum DC: 20.
 On success: choose three Final Effects. On failure: choose two Final Effects, but the disappearance is 
harsher and leaves fewer recoverable fragments. The Crew still receives a path to victory.

21. Combat Priorities
1. Is someone about to die? Use Cure Wounds, Aid, Emergency Ward Field, Flash of Genius, or defensive 
infusion/item.
2. Is there a device, pylon, anchor, lock, relay, or chain system? Use Investigation, Arcana, Tool Check, 
Shatter, Dispel Magic, Impossible Logic, or Anti-Silver Engine Lock Relay.
3. Is the battlefield cheating? Use Impossible Logic, Relay Node, Flash of Genius, Arcane Eye, or a 
prepared answer/item.
4. Is there nothing clever to do? Use Relay Node attack, Fire Bolt / Ray of Frost, Help action, or Dodge 
because Barten enjoys having organs.

24. Relic Interaction Rule
Barten can study dangerous relics. He can repair sheaths, stabilize housings, read fracture behavior, or 
build temporary containment clamps. But he cannot safely wield unstable protagonist relics unless that 
relic specifically belongs to him.
If Barten handles PLAYER’s unstable relic
He immediately risks backlash. Suggested effect: 2d6 damage at low tier, 4d6 at mid tier, 6d6+ at high 
tier, plus CON save or drop it. Flavor the damage according to the relic: frostfire, radiant chaos, root 
rupture, forge-rune recoil, shadow/paradox backlash, or Silverstrain burn.
Matthael-only note
If Matthael is present and the Frostfire Riftblade exists, Barten can understand parts of its structure, but 
cannot wield it. His gloves smoke, his skin burns, the relay screams, and he drops it with: “Ow. 
Correction. Absolutely not.”

26. One-Page Play Summary
Barten is a small earth gnome artificer and Relay Architect. He fights with a magical relay engine, not a 
rifle. He protects allies, disables devices, analyzes boss mechanics, interrupts chain systems, and 
occasionally performs impossible actions through terrifying intelligence.
He is physically weak, anxious, and constantly correcting everyone’s language. He repairs everything he 
hears, worries about problems before they happen, and loves the Crew in the least emotionally convenient 
way possible.
Across Book I, his device evolves from simple field relay into anti-silver engine lock relay, then into the 
Last Deed Relay during the Chaincaller finale.
At the end of Book I, Barten becomes the win condition. He sees that the Crew cannot win unless someone 
holds the final door from the other side. So he does.
He is not left behind. He is waiting ahead.
