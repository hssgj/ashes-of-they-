Eryn Worldroot - PLAYER Ready Crew Character Module

ERYN WORLDROOT
Playable Crew Character Module
Future Warden of Worldroot
Ashes of Thay - PLAYER-Ready Book I to Level 15 Progression
	Core fantasy: kind young druid, dangerous protector, future Worldroot guardian. He saves everyone until the roots start taking the price from him.




1. Character Purpose
Eryn Worldroot is the Crew's young druidic protector, healer, rootcrafter, terrain shaper, and emotional counterweight to the chosen PLAYER's most dangerous wounds, powers, and heroic self-destruction. If PLAYER is Matthael, this becomes the familiar frostfire catastrophe dynamic. If PLAYER is someone else, Eryn adapts to that character's pain, magic, and role in the Crew.
At the start of Book I, Eryn is not yet the legendary Warden of Worldroot. He is attractive, kind, ambitious, emotionally brave, and dangerously willing to save everyone he sees. His instinct is not "survive." His instinct is "nobody breaks while I can still stand."
This makes him beloved. This also makes him extremely easy to hurt.
Eryn's long-term arc is the transformation from a young druid trying to save people one wound at a time into the Warden of Worldroot, a protector who understands that saving everyone sometimes means becoming the root system that holds the whole world together.

3. Personality
Eryn is emotionally soft, but not weak. His gentleness is a form of courage.
Kind, but not helpless.
Beautiful in a gentle, natural way.
Young, ambitious, and emotionally open.
Slightly too ready to trust wounded things.
Horrified by cruelty, but capable of terrifying resolve.
Prone to humor when frightened.
Secretly proud when the Crew relies on him.
Very bad at letting people suffer "for strategic reasons."
He is not naive in a stupid way. He sees darkness clearly. He simply refuses to let darkness be the final answer.
Typical Lines
"You are not dying dramatically. Not today."
"PLAYER, please stop flirting with injuries."
"I can fix this. Probably. Maybe. Don't bleed faster than I think."
"Roots grow through stone. So can we."

The relationship between Eryn and the chosen PLAYER should remain flexible for each table. The affection, trust, humor, and rescue chemistry are part of the Crew tone; romance is optional and must be table-gated. If the user is playing Matthael at their own table, the Matthael/Eryn ship may be enabled. If someone else is playing, Matthael and Eryn remain Crew characters without required shipping.
PLAYER Versatility Rule

5. Origin in the Crew
Eryn joins the Crew around Icespire, where he helps against Cryovain and proves that he is not just a pretty young druid with ideals. He is brave under pressure, adaptable, and willing to stand between wounded people and something much bigger than himself.
By the time Ashes of Thay begins properly, Eryn is already part of the Crew's emotional rhythm.
He knows the jokes.
He knows PLAYER is not "fine."
He knows Mira hides fear behind sharp silence.
He knows Norbyn hears grief in stone.
He knows Barten is thinking six disasters ahead.
He knows Naevyra needs gentleness, not pity.
And he still chooses to stay.
6. Visual Design
Book I Eryn
Young male druid, attractive, gentle but strong. Lean body, expressive eyes, soft face, natural charisma. He should look like someone who belongs near roots, rain, healing herbs, and old green magic.
Clothing should be practical travel druid gear: layered cloak, worn boots, leather wraps, herb pouches, small bone/wood charms, green-brown-grey palette with subtle silver/ash accents after contact with the Crew.
Early Panaeolo Stage
The panaeolo leaf begins as a precious living cutting or seedling, carried close to his chest in a small pouch, charm, vial, or wrapped root-thread. It should feel alive.
Not cute like a pet plant. Cute like a tiny relic that might someday rewrite the forest.
Later Book I Visual Hint
Small root threads begin to climb his wrist or staff during stress. The leaf trembles when PLAYER uses dangerous signature power nearby. If Matthael is present, it also reacts to Ashen Ivy, frostfire, and Silverstrain-adjacent resonance.
7. Panaeolo Leaf / Ashen Ivy Bond
Acquisition Timing
Eryn should receive the panaeolo leaf in Surag, before or during Sella's sewer quest.
Gift from PLAYER or a Crew member - PLAYER, Matthael as Crew NPC, or another ally steals, bargains for, rescues, or 'morally relocates' the panaeolo leaf and gives it to Eryn.
Quest Reward - Sella, a sewer herbalist, druidic survivor, or corrupted garden remnant rewards Eryn with the leaf.
Chosen by the Leaf - The leaf reacts to Eryn and refuses everyone else.
Best version: combine 1 and 3. Someone finds or bargains for it, but the leaf chooses Eryn.
Emotional Core
The panaeolo leaf should connect Eryn's need to heal, PLAYER's signature wound or dangerous power, Ashen Ivy/panaeolo emotion where relevant, and the Crew's theme: nobody gets left behind.
The leaf is not just a magic item. It is the first living witness of Eryn's future.
Leaf Consciousness
The panaeolo leaf has a faint consciousness. Only Eryn can hear it. At first it does not speak in words. It communicates through warmth, root-pulses, emotional impressions, images of rain, soil, wounds, breath, and memory, and gentle warnings.
Personality of the Leaf
Curious.
Protective.
Easily frightened by chain magic.
Drawn to PLAYER's signature magic, Ashen Ivy resonance, or Crew-wound energy.
Comforted by Eryn's voice.
Hates dead soil.
Responds to Crew laughter.
It is not yet wise. It is a child-root carrying ancient memory.

9. Rootburn Mechanic
Rootburn represents Eryn overusing his bond to save others before he is ready.
When Eryn Gains Rootburn
Uses panaeolo power to prevent an ally from dropping.
Overchannels healing through the leaf.
Forces roots through dead soil or chain-corrupted terrain.
Uses Nature Sight in areas hostile to life.
Takes another creature's pain into himself.
Rootburn Track
	Rootburn
	Narrative Sign
	Effect

	1
	Hands tremble; tiny roots under skin glow green-grey.
	Disadvantage on next Strength check or Strength saving throw.

	2
	The leaf pulls too much from him.
	Speed reduced by 10 ft until short rest.

	3
	Healing begins feeding from his own vitality.
	Whenever Eryn casts a healing spell, he takes necrotic or force damage equal to spell level + PB.

	4
	The roots do not let go.
	Eryn gains one level of exhaustion after the encounter.

	5
	Deepgreen backlash.
	Eryn falls unconscious at scene end, stable but unreachable by ordinary speech for 1d10 minutes. During this time he hears the leaf clearly for the first time.



Reducing Rootburn
Short rest with care from Crew: reduce by 1.
Long rest in safe natural space: reduce to 0.
A careful Ashen Ivy or panaeolo comfort ritual offered by PLAYER or Matthael if appropriate: reduce by 1, but trigger a strange emotional vision.
Greater Restoration or similar magic: remove all Rootburn.
DM note: Do not punish Eryn for being heroic too often. Rootburn should create drama, not make the player afraid to use the character.

13. Level Progression Overview
Level 1 - Young Rootspeaker
Basic druid spellcasting, healing, nature utility, survival. Recommended cantrips: Guidance, Thorn Whip. Optional: Druidcraft, Produce Flame. Prepared priorities: Cure Wounds, Healing Word, Entangle, Faerie Fire, Goodberry, Detect Magic. Eryn heals, restrains enemies, and uses Guidance constantly because he is helpful to an illegal degree.
Level 2 - Circle Awakening
Subclass: Circle of Land - Forest, or Worldroot-flavored equivalent. Wild Shape unlocks, but Eryn is flora-first, fauna-second. Use Wild Shape for scouting, escape, small animal infiltration, or carrying messages.
Level 3 - Rootcraft Caster
2nd-level spells. Recommended: Lesser Restoration, Pass without Trace, Spike Growth, Moonbeam, Enhance Ability. Signature tactic: Spike Growth plus forced movement from allies.
Level 4 - First ASI / Feat
Recommended: War Caster, Resilient CON, or +2 WIS. War Caster fits because Eryn often concentrates while protecting others.
Level 5 - Crew Lifeline
3rd-level spells. Recommended: Revivify if allowed, Plant Growth, Dispel Magic, Call Lightning, Aura of Vitality if allowed, Sleet Storm. Signature: "No. You are not staying dead."
Level 6 - Land's Stride / Rootwalker
Movement through natural difficult terrain improves. Roots recognize Eryn as one who walks gently. Advantage on checks to navigate dense vegetation and reduced natural terrain penalties.
Level 7 - Icespire-Proven Protector
4th-level spells. Recommended: Guardian of Nature, Grasping Vine, Freedom of Movement, Divination. Eryn is now a serious Crew member, not an apprentice. Signature: "You go through me."
Level 8 - Panaeolo Candidate
ASI / feat. Recommended: +2 WIS to maximum 20, Healer, or Fey Touched. Eryn is ready to bond with panaeolo, but does not know it yet.
Level 9 - Surag / Panaeolo Bond
5th-level spells. Recommended: Greater Restoration, Tree Stride, Commune with Nature, Mass Cure Wounds. Campaign beat: In Surag, Eryn receives the panaeolo leaf. Unlock: Panaeolo Stage I - Grateful Cutting.
Level 10 - Overgrowth Protector
Ashes feature option: Overgrowth Reaction. 1/long rest, when an ally within 30 ft is hit, Eryn may cause roots to erupt and grant +2 AC against that attack or reduce damage by 1d8 + WIS modifier. Cost: 1 Rootburn.
Level 11 - Deep Healing Risk
6th-level spells. Recommended: Heal, Heroes' Feast, Transport via Plants, Wall of Thorns, Sunbeam reflavored as green-gold lifebeam if desired. Eryn can make huge saves, but major use risks cost.
Level 12 - Warden's Choice
ASI / feat. Recommended: Tough, Inspiring Leader if desired, Skill Expert: Medicine/Nature/Perception, or Resilient CON. Story choice: Does Eryn save everyone at cost to himself, or learn to let the Crew carry him too?
Level 13 - Worldroot Listener
7th-level spells. Recommended: Regenerate, Plane Shift, Fire Storm reflavored as rootfire/life-surge if desired, or Reverse Gravity as root-axis distortion if table allows. Eryn begins hearing root-memory across impossible distances.
Level 14 - Deepgreen Witness Mature Form
Once per long rest, Eryn may enter a witnessed state for 1 minute: tremorsense/blindsight 30 ft while touching natural ground, allies within 15 ft gain advantage on death saves, invisible creatures touching the same ground/root network can be sensed, and healing leaves fading green root marks. Cost: DC 15 CON save or gain 1 Rootburn.
Level 15 - Root Before Walls
8th-level spells. Recommended: Animal Shapes, Control Weather, Earthquake, Feeblemind only as nature-memory overload. Eryn can now change the shape of whole battlefields. He is not yet final Warden, but the world has started answering him.

15. Combat Playstyle
Basic Combat Loop
1. Open with battlefield control: Entangle, Spike Growth, Plant Growth, Sleet Storm.
2. Keep concentration protected.
3. Use Healing Word only when needed, not every scratch.
4. Use Thorn Whip to pull enemies into hazards.
5. Use Rootcraft for cover, bridges, restraint, or rescue.
6. Save big healing for collapse moments.
7. Accept Rootburn only when dramatically worth it.
Strengths

Strong terrain control.
Great perception/survival utility.
Powerful anti-ambush instincts.

Weaknesses
Overprotective.

Physically not a frontliner.
Vulnerable in dead soil / anti-nature zones.
Rootburn can punish reckless saves.
Emotionally baitable by wounded innocents.

17. DM Use as NPC
When used as an NPC, Eryn should not solve everything. He should heal when someone drops, warn about natural corruption, create roots/cover/escape routes, worry openly, make emotional scenes warmer, and occasionally overextend and need saving.
He should not replace player choices, negate every consequence, always have the right answer, become a flawless healer machine, or force romance.
NPC Combat Priority
1. Save dying Crew.
2. Maintain concentration control spell.
3. Protect weakest ally.
4. Detect terrain threat.
5. Use Rootburn only in major moments.

22. Optional Signature Ability
Beautiful Catastrophe Ward / Rootbound Ward
Once per long rest, when PLAYER or another chosen bonded ally within 30 ft takes damage from their own overchanneled magic, Eryn may use his reaction to reduce the damage by 1d8 + WIS modifier.
If the damage is reduced to 0, roots briefly wrap around the ally's wrist, neck, or heart. Cost: Eryn gains 1 Rootburn.
DM note: If PLAYER is Matthael and the table wants flirty Matthael/Eryn energy, keep the original name. If not, use the neutral name Rootbound Ward.
Optional Comedy Beat: Assslap-Ward
This is a table-specific comedy mechanic and should only be used if the group enjoys it. A minor protective ward accidentally triggers through affectionate physical contact, panic movement, or chaotic Crew timing.
No major combat benefit unless DM allows.
Can grant +1 morale bonus, advantage on one fear save, or break emotional tension.
Should not undermine serious scenes.
Best used once, then remembered forever with shame and affection.
Important continuity note: After this ward activates in-game, Eryn will name his bonded panaeolo plant. Do not reveal the name before the scene happens.

27. DM Notes

Do not write Eryn as fragile. He is emotionally soft, not weak. His gentleness should make him braver, not less useful.
"Breathe. I have you."
And when Eryn finally breaks, the Crew must prove they learned from him. They save him back.
