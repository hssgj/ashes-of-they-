TI, THE LAST OMEN
Companion-Sidekick Module for Ashes of Thay
PLAYER-First Versatile Edition
This module defines Ti as a major companion-sidekick entity for Ashes of Thay. Ti is not a standard 
familiar, not a full player character, and not only comic relief. Ti is an independent companion who 
bonds to the chosen PLAYER after Dragon Barrow and adapts his resonance to that PLAYER’s identity,
scars, magic, relic path, and role in the Crew.
Core design line:
Ti is small enough to hide in a sleeve, important enough to crack a prophecy, and stupid enough to lick 
the prophecy first.
1. Module Purpose
Ti is a small smoke-draconic companion born from fractured magic, dragon echo residue, 
containment trauma, and distorted Weave pressure. From Dragon Barrow onward, he becomes a key 
recurring character with his own mechanics, growth, emotional agency, and hidden story 
importance.
Ti should function as:
 combat support
 scout
 clue engine
 anti-chain reflex
 emotional anchor
 comedy valve
 future omen
 a dangerous living sign that the Weave itself has been wounded
Most importantly: Ti must be written PLAYER-first. Matthael is not the default bonded protagonist in 
player-facing modules. Matthael remains a selectable playable character, a possible sidekick/crew 
member, and a special Project IX-Lambda resonance option.

5. Ti Origin: Project IX-Lambda and the Dragon Grammar
Project IX-Lambda was the original attempt to draw a draconic ancient entity able to breach and seal 
primordial judgment into a fire genasi body. The body’s draconic cold sorcery made it a perfect match
for unstable frostfire and made it more vulnerable to Weave fracture.
That fracture manifested through Silverstrain: not simply metal, residue, or arcane filament, but 
wounded Weave forced into shape. In this origin layer, Silverstrain behaves like consciousness in 
disobedience: a refusal inside the grammar of containment. That refusal contributed to the downfall 
of Project IX-Lambda.
After Matthael escaped and hid around/after Icespire, Vaust - subordinate/projection of the 
Chaincaller - attempted another version. He tried to tie the grammar of Matthael’s breached soul to 
echoes of multiple dragons. This was not a clean rebirth. It was a second failed containment language,
a ritual grammar made from theft, echo, chain logic, and draconic residue.

9. Stable Sidekick Actions
Help: Smoke Harassment
Ti uses the Help action on one ally within 30 ft. If the Help is used against a Chain, Crown, Thayan 
construct, undead, or Weave-damaged target, and the helped roll succeeds, add +1d4 to damage, 
healing, temp HP, or progress if relevant.
Smoke Nip
Melee Spell Attack: use PLAYER spell attack modifier, or +PB +4. Range: 5 ft. Hit: 1d4 + PB force, cold, 
lightning, radiant-chaos, shadow-force, root-thunder, rune-spark, or relay-static damage depending 
on bonded PLAYER resonance.
If the target is Bloodied, chained, fractured, or magically unstable, add +1d4 once per round.
Smoke Pocket
Uses: 1/short rest at Stage II. Ti creates a small pocket of obscuring smoke around one ally within 30 ft.
That ally may immediately move 10 ft without provoking opportunity attacks, Hide as a bonus action, 
Disengage as a bonus action, or gain light obscurement until the start of Ti’s next turn. If the ally is 
Bloodied, they also gain 1d6 temporary HP.
Omen Ping
Uses: 1/short rest at Stage II. Ti locks onto a strange pressure in the scene. The DM reveals one tactical 
detail: weak point, hidden rune, fake body, chain source, trapped object, boss reaction trigger, 
emotional weakness, unstable magical object, escape route, or false illusion. The next ally acting 
directly on this information gains +1d4 to the relevant roll.
Heart Curl
Uses: 1/long rest at Stage II. Ti curls around one ally within 5 ft. That ally gains 1d6 + PB temporary HP
and advantage on their next save before the end of the scene against fear, charm, despair, 
domination, forced movement, or magical binding. If used on the bonded PLAYER while they are 
emotionally breaking, the DM may grant a short stabilizing roleplay beat.
Interact / Steal / Scout
Ti can steal a key, scratch a rune, knock over a vial, pull a tiny lever, bite a spell component pouch, 
carry a note, crawl through a crack, hide inside a cloak, spy through a keyhole, or carry a tiny object. 
Ti cannot carry heavy objects. Ti is baby, not forklift.

10. Reactions
Bite the Spell
Uses: PB per long rest. Reaction Trigger: An ally within 30 ft takes damage from a magical effect or is 
targeted by unstable magic. Ti bites the spell, rune, smoke, beam, chain, or magical pressure. Choose 
one: reduce magical damage by 1d6 + PB, reduce the save DC by 1 for one affected ally, reveal the 
source of the effect, or identify the “taste” of the magic for future recognition.
Against Chain, Crown, Silverstrain, Thayan containment, or anti-Weave magic, Ti may reveal a clue 
even if the mechanical reduction is small.
Warn Ally
Reaction Trigger: Ti notices a trap, ambush, hidden attack, or magical pressure targeting an ally 
within 30 ft. The ally gains +1d4 to AC, DEX save, Perception, or Insight against the triggering danger.
11. Ti Chaos Roll
Once per round, instead of a stable sidekick action, the bonded PLAYER may declare: “Ti rolls.” Roll 
1d20 on the Ti Round Chaos Table. This represents Ti acting through instinct, emotion, smoke￾draconic reflex, and distorted Weave fracture.
The result should always create a visible Ti moment, affect the round mechanically, and add comedy, 
danger, loyalty, clue, or omen energy.
12. Ti Round Chaos Table
1. Oh No, He Ate the Wrong Magic
Ti bites, licks, inhales, or swallows unstable magic. Ti takes 1d6 force damage and gains Unstable 
Smoke until the end of next round. While unstable, the next spell cast by PLAYER within 30 ft gains +1
damage die if it hits or the target fails. PLAYER must make a DC 13 CON save or suffer 1d4 backlash 
damage. Near Chain/Crown/Silverstrain magic, Ti gains 1 Fracture Scar.
2. Tiny Apocalypse Button
Ti touches something he absolutely should not touch. A hidden mechanism, rune, chain, seal, corpse, 
bell, crystal, wound, or villain device activates early. The DM introduces a complication immediately. 
However, Ti also reveals the source, weak point, or true direction of the danger. Bad, but useful bad.
3. Gremlin Theft
Ti steals a small object from a creature within 30 ft: key, ring, component, bolt, bullet, rune shard, 
vial, note, snack, or villain trinket. Roll using PLAYER spell attack bonus. On success, Ti gets it. On 
failure, Ti drops it somewhere inconvenient but reveals that the object matters.
4. Judgmental Smoke Face
Ti stares at one creature with extreme disgust. That creature has disadvantage on its next Deception, 
Intimidation, or Concentration check before the end of the round. If the target is pompous, noble, 
priestly, wizardly, or boss-like, PLAYER gains +1d4 to the next insult, taunt, Insight, or Persuasion roll 
against them.

5. Panic Noodle Mode
Ti wraps around an ally like a terrified smoky scarf. The ally gains +2 AC against the next attack 
before the start of next round. If the attack misses, Ti hisses triumphantly and the attacker cannot 
take reactions until its next turn.
6. Bad Snack, Good Discovery
Ti eats dust, ash, residue, bone powder, silver flakes, old paper, monster blood, or something equally 
questionable. He coughs up a symbol, smell, image, direction, or one-word omen. The DM gives one 
clue. PLAYER gains +1d4 to the next Arcana, Investigation, Survival, or Perception check related to 
that clue.
7. Dramatic Overreaction
Ti decides one enemy is the biggest threat in the room, whether or not he is correct. The next attack 
against that creature gains +1d4. If Ti is wrong, the DM must also reveal what Ti missed.
8. Smoke Tripwire
Ti stretches into thin smoke across the battlefield. The first enemy that moves more than 10 ft before 
the next round makes a DEX save vs PLAYER spell save DC. Failure: falls prone or loses 10 ft 
movement. Success: position revealed and cannot benefit from invisibility until end of turn.
9. Helpful Little Bastard
Ti uses Help with chaotic enthusiasm. One ally within 30 ft gains advantage on their next attack, 
check, or save before the end of the round. If the roll succeeds, add +1d4 damage, healing, temp HP, or
progress. If it fails, Ti loudly blames someone else.
10. Smoke Pocket
Ti creates a small obscuring smoke pocket around one ally. That ally may Hide, Disengage, or move 10
ft without provoking opportunity attacks. If Bloodied, they also gain 1d6 temporary HP.
11. Bite the Spell
Ti chomps an incoming or active magical effect. Reduce one magical damage instance within 30 ft by 
1d6 + PB, or reduce one magical save DC by 1 against one ally. If the effect is 
Chain/Crown/Silverstrain-based, Ti learns its taste and can recognize similar magic later.
12. Silver Sneeze
Ti sneezes a 10 ft cone of silver sparks. Creatures in the cone make DEX save vs PLAYER spell save DC. 
Failure: 1d6 lightning/force damage and no reactions until end of their next turn. Success: half 
damage. Allies hit are not seriously harmed, but their hair, cloak, or dignity suffers.
13. Heart Curl
Ti curls around the most wounded or emotionally shaken ally within 30 ft. That ally gains 1d6 + PB 
temporary HP and advantage on their next save against fear, charm, despair, domination, or forced 
movement before the end of the round.
14. Impossible Angle
Ti folds through a crack, shadow, pocket, chain gap, keyhole, broken rune, or smoke trail. He may 
teleport up to 30 ft and interact with one small object, lever, rune, weak point, lock, pouch, or wound.

15. Omen Eyes
Ti’s eyes flash with impossible reflected shapes. The DM reveals one major tactical truth: real enemy, 
false body, hidden spell source, boss reaction trigger, weakest defense, escape route, emotional 
vulnerability, or object that must be broken. The next ally acting on this information gains +1d4.
16. Chain-Hate Reflex
If any binding, chain, domination, control, forced teleport, or imprisonment effect is active within 60 
ft, Ti marks its source with silver smoke. The next attack, spell, or check against that source gains 
advantage or +1d6 damage, player’s choice. If no such effect exists, Ti growls at the nearest suspicious 
object and grants +1d4 to Investigation or Perception.
17. Last Omen Assist
Ti links his smoke to PLAYER’s current action. If PLAYER attacks, add +1d6 resonance damage to one 
hit. If PLAYER casts a spell, one target suffers -1d4 to its save. If PLAYER makes a check, add +1d4. If 
PLAYER is protecting a Crew member, increase the bonus to +1d6.
18. Crew Bond Flare
Ti chooses a Crew member emotionally tied to the moment. That ally may immediately use their 
reaction to move 10 ft, make one weapon attack, stabilize someone, break a grapple, impose 
disadvantage on an attack against another ally, or reroll a failed save. Ti lands on them proudly like 
he planned everything.
19. Fracture Overload
Ti opens too much of himself. Choose one powerful effect: cancel a minor forced effect, weaken a boss
effect, break a minor chain node, expose a hidden core, or give PLAYER an immediate reroll. Then Ti 
takes 1d6 psychic/force damage and gains 1 Fracture Scar. If Ti already has 5+ Fracture Scars, the DM 
may show a brief horror omen.
20. Legendary Little Shit
Ti does the impossible in the most Ti way possible. Choose one: interrupt a boss reaction, stabilize a 
dying ally at 1 HP, expose the true villain mechanism, break a key seal, steal a crucial object, give 
PLAYER advantage and +1d6 on their current action, or deal 2d6 + PB resonance thunder damage to a 
Bloodied enemy. Afterward Ti gains Exhausted Spark until comforted, fed, or short-rested.
13. Exhausted Spark
When Ti rolls a natural 20, uses a miracle-level effect, or saves someone from major scripted doom, he
may gain Exhausted Spark.
 Ti cannot roll result 20 again while Exhausted Spark is active.
 If he would roll 20, treat it as 17 instead.
 Ti’s smoke looks thin, sleepy, or cracked.
 PLAYER may remove Exhausted Spark by spending a bonus action to comfort, feed, hide, pet, 
praise, or emotionally steady Ti.
 A short rest removes Exhausted Spark automatically.

18. Fracture Intervention
Fracture Intervention is Ti’s rare anti-fate mechanic. When a major enemy or story effect causes 
forced teleport, chain bind, domination, memory rewrite, scripted doom, forced movement into 
death, no-save restraint, boss execution, or reality rewrite, Ti may intervene.
Cost: Ti gains 1 Fracture Scar.
Effect: the DM chooses an appropriate reduction. Auto-fail becomes a saving throw, failed save 
becomes reroll, restrained becomes grappled, stunned becomes dazed/disadvantage, forced teleport is
shortened or delayed, boss reaction is interrupted, chain node is exposed, one ally is stabilized, or one
doom effect is weakened for one round.
Rule:
Ti cannot erase major consequences for free. Every miracle leaves smoke-scars.

19. Stage Progression
Stage II - Loyal Little Bastard
Unlock: Dragon Barrow. Ti gains sidekick mini-turn, Ti Chaos Table, Smoke Nip, Help: Smoke 
Harassment, Omen Ping, Smoke Pocket, Heart Curl, Bite the Spell, Bond Score, and Fracture Scars.
Role: comedy utility, scout, emotional anchor, first anti-chain instincts, clue support. Visual signs: 
small smoke-draconic body, bright eyes, expressive smoke, curls around shoulders, hides in clothes, 
dramatic tiny hissing.
Stage III - Last Omen Hatchling
Unlock: after major Chain/Thay/Silverstrain scene, usually Book II. Ti gains AC +1, HP +10 or +2 per 
PLAYER level, Omen Ping becomes PB/long rest, Heart Curl becomes 1/short rest, Ti Mood Rule 
becomes fully available, and Fracture Intervention becomes fully available.
New Ability: No Bad Chain
Uses: 1/long rest. When an ally within 30 ft fails a save against restrained, dominated, frightened, 
forced teleport, forced movement, or magical binding, Ti grants a reroll. If used against a boss or 
Chaincaller-tier effect, Ti gains 1 Fracture Scar.
Stage IV - Fivefold Last Omen
Unlock: late campaign, when enemies start recognizing Ti as a key. Ti gains AC +1, fly speed 40 ft, 
Smoke Nip becomes 1d6 + PB, Bite the Spell becomes 2d6 + PB, resistance to force or lightning, and 
ability to maintain one Omen Mark.
New Ability: Omen Mark
Ti marks one enemy, object, source, chain node, or spell anchor within 60 ft. For 1 minute, the first 
ally each round gains +1d4 against it; hidden reactions from that source are revealed before 
triggering; if it attempts forced movement, control, or binding, Ti may use reaction to impose 
disadvantage.
Stage V - Awakened Echo
Unlock: endgame choice, not automatic. Ti becomes a mythic companion. He remains small most of 
the time, but his presence carries enormous weight.

23. DM Notes
Use Ti Often
From Dragon Barrow onward, Ti should appear regularly. Not in every sentence. Not as a spotlight 
thief. But enough that the party feels he is always present. Use him for clue delivery, combat assists, 
emotional reactions, anti-chain warnings, comedy breaks, and horror foreshadowing.
Ti Should Matter, But Not Solve Everything
 Ti should reveal openings, create opportunities, interrupt doom at cost, make boss mechanics 
more dynamic, and emotionally support the Crew.
 Ti should not replace PLAYER, replace the Crew, solve all puzzles alone, erase consequences 
without Fracture Scars, become full Tiamat early, or explain Silverstrain truth too soon.
Comedy Rule
Ti comedy must still be useful. If Ti does something stupid, it should also reveal a clue, distract an 
enemy, expose a weak point, move an object, create a tactical opening, or make the Crew feel alive.
Horror Rule
The scarier Ti becomes, the smaller he should feel afterward. If Ti’s smoke becomes a fivefold omen, 
let him later hide in someone’s sleeve. If Ti cancels a boss effect, let him shake afterward. If villains 
call him a key, let the Crew remind him he is family.
24. Do Not Reveal Yet
 Ti’s full connection to the final apocalypse structure.
 The true meaning of “Last Omen.”
 Whether Ti is a key, seal, witness, fang, or hearth-smoke.
 How Silverstrain recognizes Ti.
 Whether Ti can resist Dragon/Chain/Spider convergence.
 Whether Ti can remember rewritten prophecy.
 Whether Ti was accidental, chosen, or allowed to exist by something older.
 Whether Ti’s final form can affect the ending.
 The full truth of Project IX-Lambda as player-facing certainty before the story earns it.
 That Ti’s final power depends on Crew sync and not only dragon echo completion.
Keep the truth buried under behavior first. The players should first love Ti. Then fear for him. Then 
realize why everyone else wants him.
