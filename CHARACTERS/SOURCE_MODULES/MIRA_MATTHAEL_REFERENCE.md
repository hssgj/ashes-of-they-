MIRA
The Knife That Keeps the Crew Human
Project Ashes of Thay
Use Book I-II Crew Character Module
Role Rogue / Angle Witch / Shadow Executioner
Primary Function Stealth, precision damage, hard choices, Crew

Core Sentence
Mira does not lack emotion. She weaponized it until nobody could see it bleeding.
1. Character Purpose
Mira exists in the Crew as the person who does what everyone else hesitates to do.
She is not the warm heart of the party. She is the blade protecting that heart.
Where Matthael inspires, Eryn heals, Barten calculates, Norbyn anchors, and Naevyra suffers toward 
light, Mira cuts through hesitation.
Her Book I role is to end moral paralysis, protect the weak without speeches, punish enemies quickly, say 
the ugly truth, and make the Crew survive decisions they may hate themselves for later.
Mira does not lack emotion. She weaponized it until nobody could see it bleeding.
2. First Impression
Mira should enter scenes like a shadow that already decided the room was stupid. She speaks rarely. 
When she does, it lands.
Visual Identity
 dark cloak or half-cape
 practical leather armor

5. Book I Arc
Phase I - Cold Utility
At first, Mira feels like the Crew's problem-solver. She acts like kindness is inefficient, but still does kind 
things silently.
 gives Eryn bandages without comment
 drags Barten behind cover while calling him "portable disaster math"
 watches Naevyra at night so nobody else has to admit they are scared
 tells Matthael when he is being reckless instead of worshipping his chaos
"We are not saving everyone. We are saving who can still breathe."
Phase II - Moral Saver
As Book I escalates, Mira becomes the person who prevents the Crew from collapsing under guilt.
 kill a chained enemy if letting them live risks the Crew
 destroy a corrupted relic before anyone can be tempted
 threaten prisoners if necessary
 lie to enemies with no visible remorse
 stab first when mercy would become suicide
The DM should still show that she hates needing to be this person: clenched jaw, bloody hand shaking 
after the fight, cleaning her blade too long, refusing healing until others are patched, or snapping at 
anyone who thanks her.
Phase III - Barten Bond
Mira and Barten should become quietly close before the finale. Not romantic by default. More dangerous 
than that: mutual respect.

Scene Seed - Broken Mechanism
Barten is repairing a device. Mira watches.
Barten: "That part is not broken. It is disagreeing with function."
Mira: "So it is you."
Barten: "Technically, I am highly function-adjacent."
Mira: "You are lucky you are useful."
Barten: "You say that like affection wearing boots."
Mira leaves. Later, the part Barten needed is on the table. No note.
6. Book I Finale: Barten Disappears
When Barten sacrifices himself or disappears, Mira should not break immediately. That is important. She 
goes cold. Too cold.
She secures the room, checks exits, counts survivors, and asks what resources remain. Then later, when 
no one is watching, she breaks.
Breakdown Scene Seed
Mira finds a broken piece of Barten's device. At first she tries to repair it. Hands steady. Then she realizes 
she does not know the final step.
"You arrogant little bastard. You did not label this part."
She throws it. Immediately regrets it. Crawls after the pieces. Starts collecting them with shaking hands. 
This is the first time Mira visibly fails to control herself.
7. Book II Arc - The Angle Witch Awakens
Book II turns Mira from rogue into something stranger. Barten's blueprints become her inheritance.
Not "she replaces Barten." Never. She becomes the one who carries the shape of his unfinished thoughts 
into violence.
Book II Evolution Themes
 grief converted into precision
 impossible geometry
 shadow-angle movement
 gun and blade upgrades
 Barten's blueprints as emotional relic
 level-warp symptoms
 guilt over not understanding him sooner
Her New Fantasy
Mira does not teleport like a mage. She cuts through the angle between where she is and where she needs
to be.

9. Core Features
Sneak Attack
Once per turn, Mira deals extra damage when she hits with advantage or when an ally is within 5 ft. of 
the target.
Level 7-8: +4d6. Level 9-10: +5d6.
Cunning Action
Mira can Dash, Disengage, or Hide as a bonus action.
Uncanny Dodge
When an attacker Mira can see hits her, she can use her reaction to halve the damage.
Evasion
When Mira makes a Dexterity saving throw against an effect that deals half damage on success, she takes 
no damage on success and half damage on failure.

10. Signature Actions
Shadow Cut
Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
Hit: 1d6 + 4 piercing damage plus Sneak Attack if applicable.
If Mira hits a creature that damaged an ally since Mira's last turn, she may add +1d6 psychic damage 
from focused anger.
DM Flavor: The cut is not dramatic. The blood realizes it is outside the body before the victim does.
Quiet Shot
Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, range 60/180 ft., one target.
Hit: 1d8 + 4 piercing damage plus Sneak Attack if applicable.
If fired from hiding, the target must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be unable to take 
reactions until the start of Mira's next turn.
Say It Ugly
Bonus Action, 1/short rest.
Mira says the thing nobody wants to say.
Choose one ally within 30 ft. who can hear her. That ally may immediately reroll one failed saving throw 
against fear, charm, paralysis, hesitation, or magical despair. The ally must use the new result.
Example line: "You can cry after we are alive."
Hard Mercy
Reaction, 1/long rest.
When an ally within 30 ft. would hesitate, fail a death save, or be reduced to 0 HP by a creature Mira can 
see, Mira can move up to half her speed without provoking opportunity attacks and make one weapon 
attack.
If the attack hits, the ally gains temporary hit points equal to Mira's Sneak Attack dice roll.
This ability should feel brutal but protective.

Mira respects Matthael's courage but hates his self-destructive style.
Once per short rest, when Matthael casts a spell that damages multiple creatures, Mira can use her 
reaction to call an angle. Matthael may choose one ally in the spell area. That ally gains advantage on the 
saving throw.
Mira line: "Left side, genius. Try not to cook the druid."
With Eryn - Stop Saving Loudly
When Eryn heals or protects an ally while below half HP, Mira gains advantage on her next attack against
a creature threatening him.

12. Book II Upgrade Path
Stage I - Barten Blueprints
Unlocked after the Book I finale. Mira finds, inherits, steals, or reconstructs Barten's unfinished plans. She
does not understand all of them. That matters.
Feature: Blueprint Grief
Once per long rest, when Mira fails an Intelligence check involving devices, magic geometry, traps, or 
Barten's work, she may reroll the check.
If the second roll succeeds, she takes psychic damage equal to her level.
Flavor: For one second, she hears Barten explaining it. Not as a ghost. As memory sharpened into pain.
Stage II - Angle Step
Bonus Action, PB/long rest
Mira teleports up to 15 ft. to an unoccupied space she can see, but only if both her starting point and 
destination are in dim light, shadow, cover, or adjacent to a hard surface such as a wall, pillar, 
doorframe, broken machine, or battlefield debris.
At level 13+, range becomes 30 ft.
Flavor: She does not vanish. The room blinks wrong.

13. Level-Warp Symptoms
Book II should make Mira feel changed, but not instantly overpowered. Use these symptoms gradually:
 shadows bend toward her instead of away from light
 bullets leave faint geometric afterimages
 her knife sometimes exits at a different angle than it entered
 mirrors show her standing half a second late
 Barten's notes rearrange when she is angry
 she starts finishing equations she does not understand
 she hears clicks inside walls before traps activate
 when grief spikes, nearby candles burn sideways

15. DM Guidance
How to Play Mira Well
Do:
 make her quiet but not absent
 let her notice things first
 let her cut through nonsense
 show care through action, not speeches
 make her competent
 give her emotional restraint until it cracks
 make Barten's loss change her permanently
Do not:
 make her just edgy
 make her cruel for fun
 make her emotionless for real
 make her flirt by default
 make her betray the Crew casually
 make her "the rogue who steals from party"
 make her breakdown pretty
Mira's grief should be ugly, sharp, and private.
16. Do Not Reveal Yet
 Mira cares about Barten deeply before even she admits it.
 Barten's blueprints were already partly designed with Mira in mind.
 Her Book II angle-witch evolution is not normal magic.
 Her level-warp is tied to Barten's disappearance and later Paradox Engine logic.
 Her future relic path leads toward Whisperlock, Last Word, Paradox Sidearm, and eventually The 
Three Silent Stars.
 Mira's biggest fear is not death - it is being the one who survives every necessary cruelty.

17. Core Summary for Book
Mira is the Crew shadow blade, moral executioner, and silent protector. She appears cold, efficient, and 
almost emotionless, but beneath her anger is a fierce loyalty she refuses to name.
In Book I she saves the Crew by making hard choices before hesitation kills them. Her bond with Barten 
grows through mutual respect and quiet understanding. When Barten disappears, Mira does not break 
beautifully - she fractures, then rebuilds herself around his unfinished blueprints.
In Book II, that grief becomes motion, geometry, gunfire, and impossible angles. She becomes the Angle 
Witch: not because she stopped hurting, but because she learned where to cut the world so the Crew 
could keep moving.
