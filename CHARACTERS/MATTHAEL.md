# Matthael

**Type:** Player Character  
**Status:** CANON

## Crew

- Matthael is part of the Crew.
- Matthael consistently chooses Crew over his own survival.
- Matthael believes the Crew can accomplish together something that nobody can accomplish alone.
- Book I increased Matthael's determination to recover Barten.
- Matthael is willing to destroy threats that endanger the Crew.

## Ti

- Ti is Matthael's smoke-draconic familiar / companion.
- Matthael considers Ti his child.
- Ti was built from Matthael's essence.

## Riftblade

- Riftblade is Matthael's current sword and main weapon.
- Riftblade functions as a conduit for Matthael's magic.
- Riftblade is highly compatible with Matthael.
- Matthael naturally understands Riftblade's nonverbal communication.
