# Ti

**Type:** Character / Companion  
**Status:** CANON

- Ti is the recurring smoke-draconic familiar / companion.
- Ti began in a player–familiar / companion relationship.
- Matthael considers Ti his child.
- Ti was built from Matthael's essence.
- Ti began developing consciousness by imprinting Matthael's traits into his personality.
- Ti initially communicated with extremely limited vocabulary and developed language over Book I.
- Ti developed more stable emotions over Book I.
- Ti developed more stable access to his power over Book I.
- Ti's compatibility with Matthael's power developed over Book I.
- Ti became a member of the Crew before the Chaincaller fight.
- Ti functions primarily as an emotional anchor rather than a scout.
- Ti recognizes Barten's anxiety but cannot calculate or prove what is happening to him.
- Ti likes Barten and supports him emotionally.
- Ti is emotionally affected by Barten's disappearance.
- Ti senses a Silverstrain fracture in Barten's essence and therefore does not experience Barten as simply dead.
- Ti's "Not yours." moment expresses his recognition that the Crew is his and he is part of the Crew.
- Ti knows what the Crew is doing at that moment and what he must protect.
