# Chaincaller

**Type:** Enemy / Antagonist
**Status:** CANON — Book I

## Core

- Chaincaller is a bond predator.
- Chaincaller weaponizes relationships as battlefield geometry rather than merely attacking bodies.
- Chaincaller studied the Crew and learned that Matthael consistently chooses Crew over survival.
- Chaincaller's strategy therefore includes leverage, classification, isolation, and ownership.
- Chaincaller repeatedly separates the Crew through bond geometry.
- Isolation alone does not permanently defeat the Crew because they repeatedly find ways to reconnect.
- Chaincaller learns from the Crew's methods of reconnecting and begins weaponizing those connections.

## Book I / Round 2

- Chaincaller appears in Phase I as the Returned Heretic.
- At apparent defeat, the manifestation does not die.
- Silver-black chains erupt from the body and reveal a deeper Seed Host / Ixrathul layer.
- Chaincaller survives the final Book I confrontation in some form.
- Chaincaller becomes more deeply linked to Ixrathul.

## Relationship to Matthael

- Chaincaller recognizes Matthael as an anomaly and as a significant threat.
- Matthael can damage Chaincaller's manifestation.
- Chaincaller can defeat Matthael without killing him.
- Chaincaller has an interest in Matthael remaining alive.

## Future / DM Layer

- Ixrathul is not simply a stronger Chaincaller.
- Ixrathul is the deeper hunger behind the chain and uses Chaincaller as host/interpreter.
