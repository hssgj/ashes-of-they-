# Barten

**Type:** Character  
**Status:** CANON — Book I

## Identity

- Earth Gnome.
- Artificer.
- Relay Architect.
- Support engineer.
- Battlefield analyst.
- Device-breaker.
- Anti-chain technician.
- Architect of Crew survival.

## Character

- Physically small and weak.
- Extremely intelligent.
- Analytical, anxious, nervous, and physically insecure.
- Uses dry, literal, technical humor.
- His courage is acting while afraid.
- His central weakness is fear of being useless.
- Expresses affection through repairs, corrections, safety measures, and practical help.
- He is not suicidal; he is prepared, terrified, and correct.

## Capabilities

- Reads impossible systems.
- Analyzes relays and architectural errors.
- Distinguishes mechanisms from projections.
- Disrupts containment.
- Reads chain/relay logic.
- Creates anti-chain solutions.
- Predicts system failure.
- Turns system errors into new paths.
- His Impossible Read can analyze an impossible effect and provide the Crew with a response.
- Identified the Pre-Wound Signature associated with Chaincaller's attacks.

## Book I

- Prepared the Silverstrain Antichain Relay.
- The Relay required a conscious anchor.
- Barten chose himself as the anchor.
- The Relay stabilized the Crew.
- The Relay stopped the containment collapse.
- The Relay interrupted Chaincaller regeneration.
- The Relay interrupted Ixrathul influence.
- The Relay reset Chain Pressure.
- The Relay created an opportunity for the Crew's final attack.
- Barten disappeared into the Silverstrain lattice.
- Barten is known publicly as the Missing Architect.

## Player-facing end state

- Barten is gone.
- His body is not found.
- His notebook, Relay work, calculations, and traces remain.
- The epilogue contains an Empty Chair.
- Most notebook pages are burned; one page survives.
