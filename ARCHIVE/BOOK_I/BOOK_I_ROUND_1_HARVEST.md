# 0. Classification Legend

- **HARD CANON** — explicitly recovered as actual-play history or explicit creator decision.
- **PLAUSIBLE** — strongly supported but not fully verified against archive.
- **CONFLICT** — incompatible versions or naming exist; do not silently merge.
- **SPECULATIVE** — theory, hypothesis, or future concept.
- **UNKNOWN** — not reliably recoverable yet.
- **FUTURE CONCEPT** — deliberately retained for later development, not current Book I canon.
- **NEEDS ARCHIVE VERIFICATION** — memory is useful but should be checked against original project material when possible.

# 1. DRAGON BARROW

**Status: HARD CANON, with chronology/details partially unresolved.**

Recovered actual-play structure:

1. Soft combat encounter.
2. Large puzzle room.
3. Large containment room beneath it.
4. Vaerith was contained there.
5. The Crew deliberately avoided investigating/explaining the Sea of Hunger at the time.
6. After Vaerith's release, the Crew went deeper into Dragon Barrow.
7. Deeper experimental areas existed beneath/inside the Barrow.
8. The deepest experiments included the process that eventually produced Ti.

### Sea of Hunger

**Status: HARD CANON as a deliberately unresolved element; deeper meaning UNKNOWN.**

At the time of Book I, the players explicitly chose not to investigate or define what the Sea of Hunger was.

Creator note: it is intended as a long-running thread that may accompany the campaign without the Crew understanding it. It is already relevant to later material in the Glassdunes / Book II context, but its actual nature must not be retroactively declared here.

# 3. TI — ORIGIN

**Status: HARD CANON — recovered actual-play origin.**

After freeing Vaerith, the Crew went deeper into Dragon Barrow, into an experimental area where the deepest experiments were taking place.

There the process that became Ti was already being developed through:

- concept,
- essence,
- incubation,
- realization/finalization.

Matthael physically/energetically imprinted himself into a living wall or a wall carrying some form of conscience/consciousness.

That imprint triggered the finalization of Ti's incubation.

### Consequence

Ti's Book I origin is therefore directly tied to:

- Dragon Barrow,
- the experimental system,
- Matthael's imprint,
- and the completion of the incubation process.

This is the historical Book I origin and must not be replaced by a generic familiar-origin explanation.

**Status: HARD CANON for Book I behavior; origin UNKNOWN.**

- buzzing,
- vibration,
- amusement,
- disagreement,
- increasingly apparent conscience.

- **Scorch**
- **Rift Step**
- **Freeze**
