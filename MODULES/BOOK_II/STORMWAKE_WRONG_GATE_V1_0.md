SW-00 · CANON & INPUT LOCK
What wakes above Thaylambar

	
	CANON PURPOSE
Stormwake begins after the Crew reaches the surface with a true storm already created by Matthael. This module establishes the storm, lets Matthael discover that he can hear its rhythm during active danger, powers the warp ruins, and ends with the Wrong Arrival at the Temple of Kossuth.

	Controls encounter scaling and Listen costs.

	Sets emergence zones and support blocks.

	Defines intended destination signature, never exact current location.

	Surface emergence
	Shore, dunes, ruins, or split deployment.



Hard Locks
• The storm exists because Matthael deliberately routed the overload upward. Do not retcon it into weather or automatic prison failure.
• Matthael discovers that he can hear the storm here. He does not gain unrestricted control over it.
• Barten does not physically appear and the gate does not prove his location.
• The intended destination may follow Barten’s relay signature, but the gate misroutes into the Temple of Kossuth because the network is damaged and claimed by older oath-fire architecture.
• Wrong Arrival is guaranteed only after the Crew voluntarily activates the gate. Roll results determine safety, cohesion, and entry zone - not whether the next arc exists.
• The deeper Silverstrain truth remains hidden. The storm can recognize Matthael without explaining why.
Required End State
Default canon ends with the Crew transported into the Temple of Kossuth arc. The quality of the arrival, enemy trace, preserved equipment, Crew cohesion, Anaxaster debt, and Matthael’s Storm Listening milestone all remain variable.

Storm Pressure Principles
• Pulse choices should alter position, gate timing, or enemy plans - not repeatedly punish the same downed target.
• The storm does not speak in sentences. It communicates as timing, pressure, color, direction, and remembered emotion.
• An enemy storm cage can intercept one pulse. Doing so adds Interference 1 and charges Vask’s equipment.
• At Resonance 4, Matthael may redirect rather than merely survive one pulse per round, using his reaction.
Pulse Selection Examples
	Table situation
	Best pulse

	Crew clustered safely while Vask audits gate
	Chainless Wind or Glass Dune Lift to force movement decisions.

	Ti crossing to DRAC anchor
	Red-Silver Fork that may be redirected into Charge, giving risk and payoff.

	One ally already down
	White Fire Reflection or Memory Thunder elsewhere; create pressure without cheap execution.

	Enemy cages controlling field
	Lake Reversal or Chainless Wind to break cage lanes.

	Gate almost ready
	White Fire Reflection to foreshadow the wrong claim and test Alignment.

	Fractured Storm feels too punishing
	Use positional pulse with no damage, then let Resonance offer a readable warning.



Storm Memory Rule
A pulse can evoke a memory, but never inject a new factual reveal. Emotional truth is allowed; location, allegiance, and prophecy are not.

Awakening Scene
	The thunder arrives before the lightning. Then, for Matthael alone, it arrives after. The difference is less than a heartbeat and wide enough to stand inside. He realizes the storm has been warning him every time. Not with a voice. With the shape of the next wound.



Milestone Boundary
	
	STORM LISTENING, NOT STORM LORDSHIP
After this scene Matthael can recognize the rhythm of this storm and related wounded-Weave weather. He does not gain a free spell, permanent damage immunity, or universal weather control. Future mechanical feature requires an explicit milestone choice.

	Creature damage. Can stabilize one ring lane until next pulse with Arcana DC 16 after casting.

	Slows creature; no automatic storm/gate effect.

	Burning Hands
	Creature damage; may ignite dune glass into 10ft hazard.

	Fear
	Vask/Adepts affected normally unless protected; Glassbound immune if construct.

	Detect Thoughts
	Vask readable normally. Storm/gate has no mind. Barten signature yields checksum fragments, not consciousness.

	Before combat reveals anchor layout and one hidden cage; advantage first gate check.

	Counterspell
	Can stop Reclaim Conduit, Counter-Alignment, or White-Fire backlash as 4th-level effects; cannot counter gate opening.

	DC 17: suppress cage, Thayan Lock, or Oath Scar disadvantage for one round.

	Dimension Door
	Excellent regroup / transit stabilization; can auto-pass one positioning requirement.

	Teleport
	Cannot safely override destination while network is active; attempting becomes Alignment check DC 20 and Interference +1 regardless.



Storm Damage & Resistance
• Storm damage is lightning/force unless a pulse says otherwise. Existing resistance works normally.
• Silverstrain does not grant blanket immunity. It may force a roll against automatic gate displacement or route overwrite using established Defiance rules.
• A spell cannot simultaneously damage enemies, add Charge, add Alignment, and lower Interference unless a module action explicitly allows it.
Creative Magic
	
	TRANSLATE THE IDEA
A strong setup can grant advantage, replace one check, alter a pulse target, or preserve a transit asset. It cannot make a damaged multi-origin gate arrive exactly where desired without changing the story’s core premise.

The Netherese Meridian recognizes a Barten-linked correction signature in the network. It is a route checksum: proof that Barten’s work, absence, or future signal touched this family of gates. It is not a live sending and not confirmation that he waits at the destination.
