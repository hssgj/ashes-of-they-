Ashes of Thay Book II - Glass Dunes Under-Access | DM Continuity Module
Purpose
This module bridges the road from Surag and the Thaylambar outskirts toward the later Keluthar warp-gate arc. It
is the first major Glass Dunes dungeon and the first true confrontation with the mystery beneath Thaylambar 
Lake.
The Crew enters a buried under-access ruin with Red Veil as a temporary rival-guide NPC. The dungeon reveals 
that the Glass Dunes are spreading from a sealed under-lake containment axis. The final encounter leads to the 
discovery of the imprisoned entity now known, only at reveal, as Anaxaster.
DM Rule
The player should not learn the name "Anaxaster" until the final reveal in the prison chamber.
Recommended Level and Duration
Recommended Level Level 10 Crew.
Expected Duration 2-4 sessions depending on exploration depth.
Fast Route 1 long session.
Full Dungeon Route 3+ sessions.
Opening State
 The Crew left Surag after Barten’s disappearance.
 The Crew found Barten’s relay notes.
 Eryn awakened Deepgreen Witness Stage II.
 The Crew encountered the spreading Glass Dunes around Thaylambar.
 The Crew met Red Veil near an under-access ruin.
 The Crew learned that Barten passed through this area.
 The Crew learned that Red Veil is also following Barten’s trail.
 The Crew has not yet reached Keluthar.
 The Crew has not yet learned what is sealed beneath the lake.
Active NPCs and Relics
Mira - weapons still incomplete; use this dungeon for material hooks, not full awakening.
Eryn - Deepgreen Witness Stage II active.
Norbyn - Grudgebell has a voice and reacts to containment timing.
Naevyra - weapons still incomplete; radiant suppression hooks may appear.
Ti - scout, clue engine, omen behavior, chaos utility.
Red Veil - rival-guide NPC, not fully trusted.
Player-Facing Hook
The Glass Dunes are not just spreading across the land.
They are growing upward from below.
The under-access ruin descends beneath the lakeward dunes, into a buried network of pre-Thayan halls, 
Netherese relay machinery, living Rootglass, and containment seals that something has begun to disturb.
Barten went in. Red Veil followed. Neither returned by the same route.

Level 1 - Under-Access Descent
Purpose: introduce danger, dead Thayan expedition, shifting Glass Dunes, and the first dungeon mechanics.
Read-Aloud
The stairway descends beneath the Glass Dunes like the throat of something buried alive.
The upper ruin is half stone, half glass. Thayan excavation poles jut from the sand at wrong angles. Torn red 
cloth hangs from broken machinery. Supply crates lie split open, their contents fused into pale crystal.
The air below smells of dust, wet stone, and old heat.
Red Veil stops at the first landing. "From here," she says, "do not trust straight lines."
Then, far below, something knocks once. Not loudly. Deeply. The stone answers.

Central Hub - The Broken Crossroads
Purpose: major dungeon hub. Four gates: Relay, Root, Glass, Seal.
Read-Aloud
The Broken Crossroads is a circular chamber large enough to swallow a cathedral.
Four gates stand around the edge, each carved with a single word.
RELAY. ROOT. GLASS. SEAL.
At the center floats a broken mechanism of rings, crystal shards, and old metal. It rotates without touching 
anything.
Every few seconds, one ring clicks. And far below, something enormous answers.
Gate Functions
RELAY Opens route to Control Station and Barten shortcut.
ROOT Opens route to Echo Garden and safe living paths.
GLASS Opens route to transformation chambers and 
corrupted creature nests.
SEAL Opens route to Under-Lake Axis and final prison 
approach.
Main Puzzle
The SEAL gate cannot open until at least two of the other three gates are stabilized.
Stabilization options: Relay Control Station solved; Echo Garden memory accepted; Glass Surge Node defeated or 
purified.
If only one stabilization: SEAL gate opens violently. Everyone makes CON save DC 16 or takes 3d6 force + 3d6 
slashing.
If two stabilizations: SEAL gate opens safely.
If all three: Party gains advantage on first final seal puzzle check.
Glass Surge Node
Purpose: combat/puzzle hybrid in a chamber where glass dunes have punched through the dungeon ceiling and 
floor like frozen waves.
Glass Surge Objective and Checks
Objective Destroy, redirect, or stabilize the Glass Surge Node.
Arcana DC 16 Identify surge rhythm.
Nature DC 16 Eryn can soothe living roots around it.
Athletics DC 17 Physically reposition broken pylon.
Riftblade / Frostfire DC 16 Freeze-burn the surge long enough to move.
Red Veil Help Can mark weak points with blade-lines, giving +1d4 to 
one check.
Failure Consequence Each failed round: one random character makes DEX 
save DC 15 or takes 3d6 slashing.
Success Glass Gate stabilizes.

A circular abyss stretches into darkness. A center island hangs above the void, held by chains so massive they

Rootglass crawls across the floor.
Silver relay lines pulse in slow, wounded rhythm.
And below - something opens one eye.
Gold. Desert-gold. Ancient. Angry.
The entire chamber fills with heat. Not fire. Sun over endless sand.
A voice rises from the abyss.
“Who opens the prison of Anaxaster?”
