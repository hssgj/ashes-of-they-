ASHES OF THAY I – DRAGON BARROW MODULE
Overview
Level 8 adventure arc focused on Dragon Barrow, Vaerith, Ti acquisition, and the first true
containment trial.
Travel to Dragon Barrow
Skill challenge: 5 successes before 3 failures. Strange signs, silent forests, draconic markers and
Thayan traces guide the Crew.
Recognition Hall
The Barrow tests purpose rather than power. Players must define why they are here and prove
intent.
Pit Arena
Battle against the Draconic Echo. Victory comes from breaking chains and understanding the
system rather than simple damage.
Vaerith Chamber
Ancient Silver Dragon Vaerith acts as prisoner, warden and warning. Major lore reveal scene.
Lower Containment
The Consumption Below cannot be slain until understood. The entity mirrors identity and manifests
as False Matthael.
Containment Decision
Destroy, Seal, Absorb, or Rewrite. Canon outcome: Matthael absorbs the core and becomes the
new containment vessel.
Ti Acquisition
Ti emerges from the resonance node. Dragon Echo companion joins the Crew.
Freeing Vaerith
Multi-character stabilization challenge determines whether Vaerith leaves the Barrow.
Rewards
Runic Containment Body, Runic Lock, Ti, Vaerith freedom, and major Project IX revelations.
Barten Subplot
Barten secretly begins calculations for a future emergency failsafe, foreshadowing his sacrifice.
