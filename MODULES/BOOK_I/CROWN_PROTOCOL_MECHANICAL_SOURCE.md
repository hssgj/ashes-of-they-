1. CAMPAIGN ARCHITECTURE AND DESIGN RULES
Part I begins as a local rescue and ends as a declaration of war. The party learns that Cryovain and the region around Icespire were not 
the true endgame: they were noise, bait, cover, and pressure. Beneath that noise, Thay runs Project IX and the Crown Protocol.
1.1 The Three Design Pillars
Strict First, Cinematic Second
No major effect resolves without the required 5e roll. The player may describe god-tier style, but the DM first decides the mechanical hook: save, 
attack, ability check, concentration limit, action economy, spell level and consequences.
The Hunter Becomes the Bait
Every time Matthael proves stronger, Thay changes tactics. The first hunter is a construct. The next hunter thinks. Future hunters will adapt, split the
party, target allies, and exploit the containment entity.
Power Is a System, Not a Vibe
Frostfire, Khadrak-ul, Riftblade and the consumed entity are not free bonuses. They are a containment system. They can produce power, but only if 
Matthael keeps form, intent, and control aligned.
1.2 Non-Negotiable DM Rules
 If a player describes a spell effect beyond RAW, translate it into the closest legal action before resolving.
 Haste grants only the listed extra action options: Attack (one weapon attack only), Dash, Disengage, Hide, or Use Object. It does not
grant bonus-action spells.
 Concentration conflicts matter. Dropping Haste causes the normal lost-turn crash.
 Flavor can freeze the ground, light the air or shape a dome, but it does not create damage, prone, restrained, or terrain unless a roll 
and a rule support it.
 Powerful RP can grant advantage, reveal weaknesses, lower a DC, or trigger a trial response. It cannot replace the needed roll when 
danger is real.
 Every major supernatural upgrade must have a drawback, trigger, save, or future hook.

3. ACT PROGRESSION: PART I SESSION SPINE
ACT I - The Red Wagon and IX-Beta
A Thayan transport moves through a ravine. Matthael destroys the convoy, rescues Naevyra / IX-Beta, and steals the transport tube. The 
party learns Project IX has multiple designations: Beta, Lambda, Omega. Milestone: local Thay cell exposed.
ACT II - Stonehill War Room
At the inn, the tube reveals Crown Protocol, Dragon Barrow, Lower Ash Gate and the Regent. The party realizes Cryovain was not the 
point; it was pressure and cover. Hunters are expected within 24-72 hours.
ACT III - Canis Rex
The first hunter arrives: a Crown Pursuit Engine built to track IX-line anomalies. The party prepares a cemetery kill lane. Matthael 
destroys it at high cost, proving that Thay needs smarter hunters.
ACT IV - Dragon Barrow Trial
The Barrow recognizes Lambda. The test demands purpose, integration, and resolution. Matthael defeats the Draconic Echo, finds the 
Core Key, meets Vaerith, and descends to the deep containment entity.
ACT V - The Consumption Below
The entity beneath the Barrow is not evil in a simple way; it is an endless consumption function. Matthael forces it into form, defeats it, 
swallows it, and then rewrites his body with containment runes.
ACT VI - Return and Thayan Hunter Squad
The return to Stonehill is interrupted by a sentient Thayan hunter warlock and strike team. The warlock marks the entity inside Matthael, 
learns too much, and dies. Matthael integrates the sigil into his runes. Level 8 locked.
PART I MILESTONE END
Part I ends after the Barrow is resolved, Vaerith is freed, the entity is contained in Matthael, the first sentient Thayan hunter is destroyed, and the 
party reaches Stonehill for long rest. Award Level 8.

4. CORE MECHANICS AND STRICT ROLL ENGINE
4.1 Frostfire Surge Checks
Trigger whenever Matthael becomes emotionally charged, overuses power, bonds with relics, or intimidates through aura. Default roll: 
CON save DC 13-18. Success = cosmetic/environmental effect. Partial = minor leak, social reaction, small damage/pressure. Fail = 
Silver/Frostfire strain, self damage, loss of position, or hostile system notice.
4.2 Internal Containment Saves
After consuming the entity, call WIS saves when Matthael rests, is marked, reaches low HP, sees consumption magic, or deliberately 
uses the contained power. Stage 2 grants +1 to WIS saves vs internal effects and advantage when using runes defensively.
4.3 Fracture Insight
Matthael may open a controlled view into the contained entity or his fractured soul. Use Arcana vs target WIS save. Success: target is 
stunned mentally, frightened, or suffers disadvantage and cannot cast complex spells for 1 round. Fail: target gets a hook into Matthael’s 
containment.
4.4 Runic Lock (1/Short Rest)
Target an enemy, spell, area or unstable form within 60 ft. Roll Arcana against DC 15 + threat tier, or opposed spellcasting. On success, 
the target cannot teleport, disengage, dissolve, or escape form until the end of Matthael’s next turn. On major success, duration 1 minute 
with concentration-like upkeep.
4.5 Frostfire Dome / Mosaic Field
Utility cantrip shaping. Creates light, visibility control, intimidation pressure, and anti-stealth field. It does not deal damage unless paired
with a legal damaging spell. Base DC 13 Arcana to create, DC 18 to make it mechanically relevant (e.g., enemy stealth disadvantage, 
ally movement lanes).
4.6 The Containment Host
Matthael carries the consumption function. It is not a familiar, not a patron, not an ally. It is a hostile utility function under lock. It can 
empower control magic, but every major use should invite a WIS or CON save after the scene.

5. ENCOUNTER MANUAL
5.1 Ravine Convoy Raid
Objective: stop Thayan transport, rescue IX-Beta, steal documents. Key checks: Stealth DC 15 to approach, Arcana DC 15 to read target 
mind/warding, attack rolls for wagon disables, Athletics/Acrobatics DC 14 for flood escape. Complication: if one scout escapes, Canis 
Rex arrives 1 day earlier.
5.2 Stonehill Tube Opening
Objective: decode Crown Protocol. Key checks: Arcana DC 15 safe open, Investigation DC 14 route lattice, Insight DC 15 to infer 
Cryovain as exploited pressure event. Reveal: IX-Beta retrieval, IX-Lambda active, IX-Omega Regent Vessel, Dragon Barrow dormant 
echo.
5.3 Cemetery Ambush: Canis Rex
Objective: destroy pursuit engine before it reaches town. Setup: one primary lure, two flanks. Recommended terrain: broken graves, 
mausoleum walls, root lanes. First round matters: anchors or pounce can force grapple. Win condition: destroy core/head or disable 
anchors and burn out systems.
5.4 Dragon Barrow Trial
Objective: be evaluated, not merely fight. Tests: Purpose, Integration, Resolution. Wrong answers spawn defense. Correct answers open 
deeper levels. Reward: Core Key, access to Vaerith.

Removed

10. CONTINUITY HOOKS INTO VOLUME II
The Hunter Escalation
Canis Rex was hardware. The warlock was analysis. The next hunter should be doctrine: a team built specifically around Matthael’s observed 
decisions.
Thaymount Route
The transport tube, hunter directive, and Barrow resonance point toward Lower Ash Gate / Thaymount Lower Ascent.
IX-Omega
Omega is not yet confirmed active. The party should fear that Matthael’s success accelerates it.
Vaerith’s Call
Vaerith grants a one-time call. Use only when the burden is too heavy or when draconic memory is required.
The Contained Hunger
The entity is silent after Stage 2, not gone. Let it react to hunger, reality damage, void magic, and attempts to extract or copy Matthael.

Cryovain was exploited as regional pressure. Its corpse, memory or echo can still matter as bait, sacrifice, or comparison point.
