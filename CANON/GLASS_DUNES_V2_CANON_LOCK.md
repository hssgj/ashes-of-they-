3. Route Logic by Act
Act I — The Dungeon That Notices
Completed. Entry, relay systems, Rootglass adaptation, and Ti’s SEAL contact establish that the prison is no longer passive.
Narrative question: What kind of structure rewrites life instead of merely killing it?
Mechanical payoff carried forward: dormant Seal Alert is active.
Continuity correction: Barten clues from this act are echoes, not a trail of recent physical travel.
Act II — The Living Branches
Completed. Rootglass, Echo Garden, Ivy Keeper, Caern, Nessie, collars, and the Froghemoth expand the original module into a full middle act.
Narrative question: Who learned to bind animals before scaling the same logic up to dragons, people, memory, and reality?
Eryn learns that Rootglass carries coercion; Caern recognizes leash architecture; Matthael recognizes prison logic.
The Froghemoth is the final beastbinder-scale proof before the dungeon reveals city- and dragon-scale containment.
Act III — The Bridge That Commits

	Regroup, expose the scale below, resolve who follows which route, and give the Crew one last readable choice before the Chain Spine.

Act IV — The Machine Beneath the City
The Maintenance Spine and Chain Arena translate the old module’s buried-city and anti-seal material into a playable approach rather than a single skill challenge.
Maintenance Spine: quiet dread, Barten Echo, layered-builder proof, route preparation.
Chain Arena: active initiative encounter with chain-lock nodes, pressure hazards, guardians, and structural stability.
Success is not “deal enough damage.” Success is “disable the prison’s definition of the Prisoner while keeping an exit alive.”
Act V — The Wrong Prisoner
The Prisoner is revealed only in the final chamber. The name Anaxaster becomes valid in-character at that moment.
He is wounded, furious, intelligent, and not automatically ally or villain.
The Crew may bargain, confront, or attempt a controlled release.
The module must steer toward release without removing agency: refusing or mishandling the release changes cost and relationship, not the existence of the larger pressure crisis.
Act VI — The Storm That Must Be Made
The prison’s failure creates power and danger, but not the true storm by itself. Matthael creates the storm by directing the overload upward.
1. Chain-lock containment fails enough for Anaxaster to move.
2. The deeper hunger-force recognizes Silverstrain in Matthael’s Frostfire.
3. Matthael rejects the system’s logic rather than accepting its assigned role.
4. Crew members hold architecture, timing, roots, routes, and pressure long enough for him to act.
5. A sky vent is opened or torn through the lakeward structure.
6. Overload is sent upward; only now does the true storm form.
Act VII — The Wrong Gate
The dungeon ends on the surface only after the storm is born. The arc then uses the storm to reach the warp ruins and misroute into the Temple of Kossuth, preserving the corrected Book II order.

PATCH 03
BARTEN ECHO ALIGNMENT
Silverstrain Relay Imprint Rules

1. Definition
Barten Echo Alignment is a prison-reality phenomenon created when four things overlap: the under-lake relay network, wounded Weave pressure, Silverstrain in Matthael’s Frostfire, and Barten’s connection to the wider prison / gate architecture.
	
	IT IS NOT BARTEN
The echo is not a ghost, clone, sending, time-travelling body, hidden prisoner, or proof of recent physical presence. It is a temporary alignment in which Barten’s absence, prior work, sacrifice, possible future signal, and chain-memory are rendered as if they occupy the same place.



2. What the Echo Can Reconstruct
Barten’s handwriting, verbal rhythm, humour, habits, and problem-solving style.
A room that appears recently used: a mug, maps, sleeping roll, scattered tools, equations, footprints, or warmth.
Corrections Barten made elsewhere in the network, or may make when another part of the network aligns.
True architectural relationships: wrong target classification, unsafe sequences, route timing, network contradictions.
A brief Barten-shaped figure or voice that reacts within a narrow subject boundary.
3. What the Echo Cannot Establish
Barten’s exact present location.
Whether Barten is alive at the precise moment of the echo.
Whether he physically visited the current room.
When or how the true reunion happens.
The full identity of the deeper hunger-force.
The Prisoner’s name before the reveal chamber.
Red Veil’s hidden allegiance.
A guaranteed future. Echoes can contain possibility and temporal overlap, not prophecy-grade certainty.
4. Core Behaviour Rules
	Truth without timestamp
	An echo does not intentionally lie, but a true statement may belong to another time, location, or possible alignment.

	No durable campsite
	Echo-created personal objects fade into silver frost, ash, static, or inert Rootglass when carried beyond the site.

	No free conversation
	The echo answers only within the local subject: route, seal, chain, relay, prisoner classification, or immediate danger.

	No independent mind
	Detect Thoughts finds no normal consciousness. Sending cannot use the echo as a target.

	Emotional accuracy
	It may feel painfully like Barten because the network reconstructs manner and relationship, not because he is secretly standing there.

	Pressure cost
	Forcing a stronger alignment increases prison pressure or causes psychic / Frostfire backlash.



5. Echo Intensity Tiers
	TIER
	FORM
	TABLE EFFECT

	TIER I
	Residue
	Handwriting, footprints, warm mug, moved tools, smell, a single repeated line. No direct response.

	TIER II
	Alignment
	Voice or partial figure answers one narrow prompt, repeats a correction, or reacts to a local action.

	TIER III
	Relay Imprint
	Brief interactive overlap lasting up to 1 minute. One meaningful clue can be extracted before collapse.

	TIER IV
	Forbidden Convergence
	A seemingly full reunion. Do not use in Glass Dunes; reserved for later stable-contact arcs.



6. Recognition Checks
	Perception DC 14
	Notice impossible details: reflection lag, footprints beginning nowhere, steam without heat source, dust not disturbed by the figure.

	Insight DC 15
	Recognize that responses are constrained and assembled around a local problem rather than produced by a fully present mind.

	Arcana DC 16
	Identify relay / wounded-Weave alignment. Confirm that this is not normal illusion, sending, or undead manifestation.

	Nature DC 15
	Eryn / Deepgreen Witness senses no living root-memory in the Barten shape; the environment is being forced to imitate presence.

	Ti Omen Ping
	Ti reacts to the empty space behind or around the echo, not to Barten as a living creature.

	Detect Thoughts
	No mind. The caster hears overlapping fragments and must make WIS save DC 15 or take 1d6 psychic damage.



7. Active Alignment Mechanic
	
	ONE SITE, ONE REAL CLUE
Each prepared echo site contains one major actionable truth. The Crew may obtain it automatically by engaging the scene carefully, or attempt to force clarity through a check. Failure creates cost, not a false canon clue.



	Stabilise
	Arcana, Insight, Investigation, or Nature DC 16. A relevant Barten note, Deepgreen Witness, Grudgebell timing, Ti omen, or careful relay tool use can grant advantage at DM discretion.

	Success
	Receive the site’s major clue. One character gains advantage on the next directly related prison check.

	Failure
	The clue arrives fragmented but still truthful; no advantage. Trigger one backlash: 2d6 psychic, loss of reaction until next turn, or +1 Prison Pressure.

	Overchannel option
	Matthael may force automatic clarity by accepting 2d6 psychic damage and +1 Prison Pressure. This is not spellcasting and cannot be reduced to zero by clever wording.

	Repeat attempts
	No. Once the site collapses, further rolls only produce static and emotional residue.

	Interrogation limit
	At Tier III, ask up to three questions, but only one answer contains new actionable information. The other answers are flavour, warning, or temporal ambiguity.



8. Allowed Clue Categories
A safe sequence, timing window, or route correction.
Proof that the network is classifying the wrong subject as the primary danger.
Proof that Thayan and Netherese systems were added to an older containment structure.
Proof that beast collars, chain-locks, and gate relays share command logic.
A warning that destroying every chain will collapse the Crew’s exit.
A hint that pressure can be routed upward through an architectural vent.
A Barten-style warning about Matthael using unstable machinery as a pipe, weapon, argument, or all three.
9. Forbidden Answers
“Barten is alive and located at X.”
“Barten will return in Book / chapter X.”
“The deeper force is [full identity].”
“The Prisoner is Anaxaster” before the reveal chamber.
“Red Veil works for [full faction / master].”
Any answer that makes the coming release, storm, or Wrong Gate feel pre-recorded and unavoidable.
