# ASHES OF THAY

# GLASSDUNES RETOLD

## Replay Script — Canon Reconstruction

### PRINCIPLE

This is not a transcription.

This is a replay of the original Glassdunes dungeon built around its surviving canon.

The important scenes, revelations, consequences, creatures, mechanics and recoverable declarations remain fixed.

Matthael's exact actions between those anchors are played again.

If the new play creates a better line, stunt, spell interaction or Crew moment without contradicting the old outcome, it becomes part of Glassdunes Retold.

---

## CAST LOCK

### MATTHAEL

Frostfire sorcerer-warrior.

Cracked skin, ashen hair, damaged clothing, Frostfire blade.

Chaos first, engineering second, self-preservation somewhere around seventh.

His comedy should vanish instantly when he becomes genuinely angry.

Frostfire is not generic blue fire. It can express heat, cold, fracture, pressure and the strange Silverstrain-adjacent resonance Matthael does not yet understand.

Do not prematurely explain Silverstrain.

---

### ERYN

Druid.

The party's ecological conscience and frequent victim of Matthael Engineering™.

His importance rises sharply in the greenhouse because he is the character who first understands:

growth is not the same as corruption.

---

### MIRA

Rogue.

Fast, observant, competent.

Usually the first to notice mechanical patterns while everyone else is making the situation worse.

Dry counterweight to Matthael.

---

### NAEVYRA

Active Crew member from the beginning.

Sharp.

Passive-aggressive.

Capable.

Never reduced to “the woman who needs rescuing.”

Even while restrained or endangered she should still feel like Naevyra.

---

### TI

Tiny smoke dragon.

Very simple vocabulary.

Short thoughts.

Short sentences.

Examples:

“Bad.”

“Old.”

“Can bite?”

“No water.”

“Big frog.”

“Matthael stupid.”

He can perceive strange things, but does not suddenly become an academic lecturer.

---

### CAERN

Ranger / druid.

Not fully Crew.

Reluctant hostile ally / cameo forced deeper with them by the dungeon.

Creates and reshapes wooden fighting staffs using druidic growth.

Fieldcraft, animal sense, natural magic.

He should resist getting absorbed into Crew nonsense.

---

### NESSIE

Caern's dog.

Always physically present unless there is a specific reason she cannot be.

She smells danger, reacts to strange roots, enters fights and generally demonstrates better survival instincts than Matthael.

---

### NORBYN

Dwarven smith.

Practical perspective.

Good for identifying construction, stress, metalwork and anything Matthael is about to destroy irresponsibly.

---

## ACT I — UNDER THAYLAMBAR

### PURPOSE

Introduce the Crew organically.

Establish Glassdunes as something stranger than “evil dungeon.”

The reader/player should initially assume:

ancient ruin beneath lake = dangerous abandoned complex.

That assumption slowly dies.

---

### OPENING IMAGE

Lake Thaylambar above.

Then descent beneath it.

Water overhead.

Buried city architecture below.

Glass-like roots threaded through stone.

Red modifications intruding into older green/rootglass structures.

Matthael notices that some parts feel grown.

Others feel installed.

Do not explain why yet.

---

### COMEDY SPACE

Heavy Crew banter allowed.

Naevyra already present.

Caern annoyed by everyone.

Ti simple.

Nessie suspicious of things before humans are.

Important tone:

the Crew treats nightmare archaeology like a badly managed work shift.

---

### CINEMATIC FORESHADOWING

Somewhere far below:

THUM.

Not a monster roar.

Not yet.

Pressure.

Matthael's Frostfire responds slightly.

He cannot identify why.

No “Silverstrain” exposition.

No Anaxaster name.

No true storm.

---

## ACT II — ROOTGLASS KENNEL

### FIXED ENCOUNTER

The dungeon's red-control layer becomes explicit.

Handler / leash apparatus.

Naevyra is involved in the encounter but remains an active combatant/personality, not a passive prop.

Rootglass-Collared Froghemoth rises from the flooded section.

The creature is being controlled.

But unlike the previous prose draft:

CANON OUTCOME:

THE FROGHEMOTH DIES.

Its death leads directly into the Rootglass Kennel aftermath and harvest.

---

### RECOVERED CINEMATIC MATTHAEL LINES

When Caern is dragged into helping:

«“Whack that whack.”»

And:

«“You can beat me up later.”»

When Froghemoth reveals itself:

«“You ugly.”»

Beat.

«“Really ugly bastard.”»

Those should survive unless Replay-Matthael naturally produces something even more Matthael during play.

---

### ENCOUNTER LOGIC

Important targets remembered from the original encounter:

- control rings / leash mechanism
- flooded anchor
- Froghemoth collar-core

Exact tactical order may be replayed.

Do not force previously novelized actions such as Ti biting off a finger unless Matthael actually tells him to do something equivalent during replay.

---

### POSSIBLE ORIGINAL DECLARATION

Recovered:

«“Bite that ring off the finger and bring it to me.”»

If this situation naturally appears, preserve it.

Ti's response should remain simple.

Something like:

«“Can bite.”»

Not elaborate dialogue.

---

### MAGIC

Frostfire can be driven into the Froghemoth encounter.

A surviving version includes Frostfire affecting its mouth/jaws.

Eryn uses thunder during the fight.

The exact choreography is replayable.

---

### END STATE — HARD ANCHOR

Froghemoth dead.

Crew survives.

Kennel falls quiet.

Then comes loot and investigation.

Recovered harvest includes:

- cracked collar-core
- rootglass-infested hide
- nerve/tongue sinew
- bile sac
- teeth
- broken clasp
- old civic seal

These items do not all need equal screen time.

The civic seal matters because it reinforces:

this was once infrastructure belonging to something larger.

Not simply a monster kennel.

---

## ACT III — CAERN CANNOT LEAVE

After the Froghemoth, Caern has every reason to leave.

He tries.

The prison does not permit clean separation.

Passage / geometry / containment response effectively returns or traps him with the Crew.

This is important.

Caern is not suddenly loyal.

He is still:

“Apparently I am stuck with these idiots.”

Matthael will naturally find this hilarious.

Caern will naturally not.

---

## ACT IV — ROOTGLASS GREENHOUSE

### REVEAL

The Kennel was built over or into something older:

a submerged greenhouse / former city infrastructure / research ecology.

The red leash system is not the original purpose.

Living containment ecology predates it.

Glass and growth are not inherently evil.

---

### ENCOUNTER

Rootglass Leash-Mite Swarms ×3

Original mechanical skeleton:

- AC 14
- HP 36 each
- climb movement
- Leash Prick
- WIS DC 14
- swarm can split when damaged

Collar-Nest Node

- AC 15
- HP 30
- acts/pulses around initiative 20
- regenerating/control function

Environment:

- rootglass water = difficult terrain
- cages / ruins = partial cover
- hanging vines
- node integrated into the greenhouse

---

### RECOVERED MATTHAEL MOMENT

Before an attack:

«“Watch this, twig-boy.”»

Target likely Caern.

Then Scorch / Frostfire interaction hits the strange ecology.

And the dungeon answers unexpectedly.

ACCIDENTAL HASTE

Matthael becomes magically accelerated.

This was real.

Not invented for the novel.

It should be a great “oh shit” moment.

Matthael did something stupid—

and the dungeon rewarded him.

Which is objectively terrible for his long-term behavior.

---

### RECOVERED JOKE

During the Rootglass interaction:

«“I promise I didn’t think of race, Rootie.”»

Keep the context organic.

Do not force it somewhere nonsensical just because the line survived.

---

### PARTY ACTIONS — SURVIVING VERSION

Caern + Nessie engage a swarm.

Naevyra and Norbyn help expose the Node.

Ti contributes his extremely sophisticated magical judgment:

«“Bad.”»

His smoke-face / reaction interferes with the Node.

Perfect Ti.

Keep it.

---

### MID-FIGHT STATE FROM ORIGINAL PLAY

A surviving battle state had:

- Swarm A heavily damaged
- Swarm B injured
- Swarm C nearly dead
- Node damaged
- Matthael Hasted

Later:

- Swarms A and C destroyed
- Swarm B almost dead
- Node at roughly 6 HP
- Riftblade hilt jammed into/exposing the Node
- Caern leash-marked but not controlled

We don't need to recreate HP totals exactly unless useful.

The important cinematic structure is:

Matthael accidentally becomes frighteningly fast while everybody tries to kill the Nest before it can communicate.

---

### BARTEN ECHO

Barten is NOT physically present.

Only Echo Alignment.

Recovered warning:

«LEASH = TEST
NODE = TONGUE
CUT BEFORE IT SPEAKS»

This is the first strong indication that these mechanisms are doing more than restraining wildlife.

They communicate.

Or report.

---

## ACT V — THE DEEPGREEN WITNESS

After the violent leash ecology comes something quieter.

This section belongs largely to Eryn.

The Crew reaches older greenhouse systems.

Something preserved there can distinguish the state of growth.

Core concepts:

NATURAL

ARTIFICIAL

FORCED

This distinction becomes a major Glassdunes theme.

A monstrous organism may be natural.

A beautiful structure may be forced.

A root can be altered without being enslaved.

A leash is not defined by its material.

It is defined by what choice it removes.

---

### ERYN MOMENT

Recovered formulation:

«“I won't repair you into a shape useful to me.”»

Beat.

«“I'll ask.”»

That is the emotional center of this section.

The Witness responds.

Eryn gains the ability/context to perceive the difference between natural, altered and forced growth.

No massive lore speech.

Let the room show it.

---

## ACT VI — BLACKROOT / THE ROOTS BENEATH THE LOCK

Tone turns darker.

The greenhouse ends.

Containment infrastructure begins.

Blackroot becomes visible.

The Crew investigates.

The feeling should change from:

someone experimented here

to:

someone built systems on top of systems because the previous systems were not enough.

---

### SILENT RELAY

A surviving response:

«“Food is too small a word.”»

Do not immediately explain this.

It should land horribly.

---

### HARD CHECKPOINT

THE ROOTS BENEATH THE LOCK

This is a real canonical checkpoint.

At this point:

- Crew is still beneath the lake
- Anaxaster has NOT been released
- no true storm yet
- no premature apocalypse
- no combat/release has automatically begun
- the Crew is investigating containment
- Barten remains only an echo/alignment trace

The central presence is still mostly:

breath

pressure

chain tension

black ichor

weight

---

### IMAGERY

A vast mechanism.

Chains disappearing into darkness.

A Breath Collar constricting.

A Weight Crown descending.

Something beneath all of it shifts.

The prison reacts like a living machine.

Recovered response from the deeper system:

«“Food is too small a word.”»

---

## ACT VII — FIRST CONTACT

Matthael moves toward the chain system.

Frostfire interacts with it.

The water around a chain pole freezes during one approach.

The system does something extremely important:

IT RECOGNIZES HIM.

Not attacks.

Not welcomes.

Recognizes.

Ti senses it too.

Recovered simple Ti response:

«“...I know that.”»

---

### DETECT THOUGHTS / CONTACT

Matthael attempts contact through the chain.

Recovered fragment:

«“...you... carry...”»

Then fragments:

KHAR-SULL

TAKEN

BOUND

NOT SLEEP

Ti understands one thing first.

«“He isn't sleeping.”»

Eventually:

«“Anaxaster.”»

The name lands before the complete truth does.

---

### FAILED COMMUNICATION BREACH

Matthael pushes too hard through Frostfire / Arcana.

This is one of the important cinematic failures.

A catastrophic resonance.

THUUUUUUUUUUNNNNNNG.

Chains snap taut.

Frostfire briefly dies or gutters.

A colossal glowing eye opens in the darkness.

Not merely angry.

Hungry.

Consumption.

And then:

«“...Matthael...”»

Now it knows his name.

Or knew it already.

Do not resolve which.

---

## ACT VIII — ANAXASTER

The Crew eventually sees what is actually imprisoned.

Anaxaster.

Ancient.

Chained.

Connected structurally to the prison.

Not simply locked inside it.

He is part of how it functions.

---

### CHAIN INVESTIGATION

Matthael begins loosening / interrogating the chain system.

Recovered sequence includes an attempt with mixed failures and successes.

One important success:

a red-chain link loosens.

Anaxaster responds:

«“Ask who gave it my name.”»

Excellent line.

Preserve it.

---

### KHAR-SULL REVEAL

Further chain work.

Anaxaster:

«“I was moved…”»

Then:

«“From Khar-Sull.”»

Critical concept:

The prison was altered so that it would believe Anaxaster belonged here.

His binding was relocated.

His role rewritten.

He was made into part of the system.

---

### MATTHAEL'S INTERROGATION

Core questions to preserve in spirit, preferably wording where natural:

«“Tell me why and how they moved you.”»

«“What are they holding under your weight?”»

And eventually:

«“Why are you not guardian—but prisoner of the lock?”»

---

### ANAXASTER TRUTH

Recovered:

«“They moved the seat of my binding.”»

And:

«“They are holding the thing that killed my kingdom.”»

Then the important distinction:

«“I was never its guardian…”»

«“I was its prisoner.”»

Another recovered formulation:

«“What lies beneath us is not another prisoner. It is what the prison was built to keep from becoming one.”»

That is the real horror reveal.

Anaxaster is dangerous.

But he is not the deepest problem.

---

### CREW RESPONSES

Recovered Naevyra line:

«“Please wait until after you've finished interrogating the ancient desert king before you let the unknowable thing beneath reality eat you.”»

Extremely Naevyra.

Keep.

Eryn:

«“The roots felt something… And they don't like it.”»

Ti:

«“Old. Hungry.”»

Perfect.

No extra vocabulary needed.

---

## ACT IX — THE CHOICE TO FREE THE KING

This should NOT happen immediately after meeting Anaxaster.

We need tension.

Questions.

Chain investigation.

Arguments.

Understanding what each restraint actually does.

The Crew must realize:

freedom and containment are mechanically entangled.

Breaking a chain is not just opening a lock.

It redistributes load.

---

### BARTEN

Barten remains absent.

Only traces.

His role is to make Matthael distrust simple answers.

Any warning used here should reinforce:

Do not assume the first speaking prisoner is the whole story.

Do not assume “freeing the captive” equals “solving the dungeon.”

---

## ACT X — CONTROLLED CATASTROPHE

Eventually Matthael commits.

Not because he suddenly knows everything.

Because he believes he understands enough to act.

That distinction matters.

The final internal restraints are attacked.

MATTHAEL MELTS THE NODE / INTERNAL CHAINS.

Anaxaster is released from the structure.

The load changes.

Everything that had been distributed through the prison suddenly needs somewhere else to go.

Now—

and only now—

the true storm begins.

---

## ACT XI — FROSTFIRE CHIMNEY

Pressure rises.

Not metaphorically.

The Glassdunes begin failing.

Silver-black force moves through buried infrastructure.

Down is unacceptable.

So Matthael creates another route.

UP.

He uses Frostfire to cut/form a vertical release shaft toward Lake Thaylambar.

Anaxaster assists / braces the collapsing system.

Recovered instruction:

«“Make it straight.”»

The chimney becomes both escape route and pressure vent.

This is the dungeon climax.

The Crew climbs while Glassdunes tears itself apart underneath them.

Rootglass flashes.

Old systems break.

Water breaches.

Frostfire runs over Matthael's body.

His skin becomes visibly cracked and luminous.

---

## ACT XII — THE SURFACE

The Crew erupts out onto Lake Thaylambar.

Black-and-silver weather above.

The storm is the consequence of what just happened below.

Not a random earlier effect.

Anaxaster is finally outside.

The Crew barely has time to understand that they survived.

Then—

someone is waiting.

---

## ACT XIII — WELCOMING CEREMONY

Recovered Matthael declaration:

«“Heh... just came out and we already have welcoming ceremony.”»

Matthael raises his hand.

Creates an ice bridge across Thaylambar.

Charges the figure.

This confrontation is fast and cinematic.

Afterward Frostfire crawls across Matthael's damaged body, sealing injuries while leaving the characteristic glowing cracks.

Recovered line:

«“Detention expired, fella.”»

Matthael jumps down directly in front of the surviving opponent.

«“Tell me who sent you…”»

Beat.

«“And you might get out in one piece.”»

---

## ACT XIV — THE BLACK GEYSER

Before the interrogation can resolve—

Thunder from below.

A huge black geyser erupts from the shaft.

Anaxaster immediately shields the Crew and forces/protects them toward shore.

Matthael looks at it.

Realizes the implications.

Recovered declaration:

«“So lemme guess…”»

«“You're that mean black thing buried down there…”»

«“Now it's gonna eat up whole lake and do apocalypse on coastal cities…”»

Then:

«“Whell… what a successful dungeon.”»

Perfect ending energy.

---

## EPILOGUE

Everyone else is processing:

- Anaxaster is free.
- Glassdunes is broken.
- something deeper responded.
- black pressure is erupting into Thaylambar.
- Barten's trail continues.
- the entire containment balance may have changed.

Matthael sits/melts down beside Eryn.

Lights Ashen Ivy.

Looks at the lake.

Then remembers something.

«“I have melt the node…”»

Pause.

«“It will redistribute, right?”»

Cut.

END — GLASSDUNES RETOLD
