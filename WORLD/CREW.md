# Crew

**Type:** Established Group / Core Relationship Structure  
**Status:** CANON — Book I

## Core

> Crew is Crew because they believe they can accomplish together something that nobody can accomplish alone.

## Structure

- The Crew originally arose during solo play as a practical solution to missing character capabilities.
- In the story, the group became a real Crew rather than remaining a sidekick system.
- Each Crew member has a different specialization.
- Crew members' abilities complement one another.
- Matthael consistently chooses Crew over his own survival.
- Chaincaller studied this behavior and weaponized it.
- Crew members repeatedly risk their lives for one another.
- Crew members do not want to leave another member to die.
- The Crew becomes weaker in some respects when individual members are absent.

## Voice

- Core tone is caring but lightly insulting.
- Crew voice uses humor, irony, light insults, and care masked as teasing.
- The Crew has been described as "spolutyrani ironie".

## Development through Book I

- Book I moved the Crew toward actively choosing one another. This remains a plausible synthesis rather than a separate locked event.
