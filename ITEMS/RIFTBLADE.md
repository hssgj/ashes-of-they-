# Riftblade

**Type:** Item / Weapon  
**Status:** CANON

- Riftblade is Matthael's current sword and main weapon.
- Riftblade has a magical consciousness.
- Riftblade communicates through vibration, magic, and ethereal feeling rather than ordinary speech.
- Matthael naturally understands Riftblade's communication.
- Riftblade is highly compatible with Matthael.
- Riftblade functions as a conduit for Matthael's magic.
- Established abilities: Rift Step, Scorch, Freeze.
- Riftblade is made from silver derived from the bone of Matthael's dragon.
- The material and established history of the sword were supplied as source evidence for continuity.
