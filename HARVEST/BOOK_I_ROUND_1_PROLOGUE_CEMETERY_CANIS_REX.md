## 1. EVENTS / SEQUENCE

### Prologue — Thayan Facility
- Matthael's earliest recovered playable sequence is a Thayan-facility escape.
- The facility contains glass, metal, magical systems, machinery / boiler infrastructure, containment structures, guards and casters.
- Matthael is physically unstable and visibly carries Frostfire-related damage / energy: cracks, smoke, glowing areas and unstable power.
- Matthael attempts to recover his Frostfire Riftblade.
- He escapes through destructive and improvised movement rather than following a clean route.
- During the escape he discovers a severely injured woman: Naevyra.
- He chooses to help her rather than leave her behind.
- He carries her through the facility and uses improvised methods to move her safely.
- He overloads / sabotages the facility's boiler / machinery system to create an escape route.
- The facility begins to burn and fail around them.
- They escape the facility together.
- Matthael treats Naevyra and continues moving with her despite her condition.
- They are pursued by Thayan forces / hunters.
- Matthael repeatedly prioritizes keeping Naevyra alive and away from incoming attacks.
- Chaincaller eventually enters the sequence.
- Matthael confronts / provokes him but does not defeat him.
- Chaincaller overcomes Matthael and takes Naevyra.
- Matthael is left unconscious / defeated.
- This sequence later became established as a prologue / nightmare / memory rather than the current timeline.

### Transition
- A significant time gap follows the facility / Chaincaller sequence.
- Matthael survives.
- The main adventure eventually continues in the Icewind Dale / Icespire context.

### Early Main Timeline
- Matthael is travelling with the emerging Crew.
- The Crew's early period contains investigation, travel, banter and dangerous encounters.
- **Naevyra is NOT with the Crew during the Cemetery sequence. She joins only after the Crew rescues her there.**
- **Ti belongs to the later harvest and is intentionally excluded from this section.**

### Cemetery / Barrow / Canis Rex sequence
- The recovered early adventure leads the Crew toward a cemetery / crypt / barrow location.
- The location contains evidence of a larger magical / technomagical control network.
- The Crew encounters Canis Rex.
- Canis Rex is intentionally treated as a dangerous, above-party-level encounter rather than an ordinary beast.
- The encounter escalates into a second stage / transformed state.
- Matthael identifies that Canis Rex is connected through a vein / string / leash mechanism to a deeper system.
- Matthael uses Frostfire creatively to attack or expose that connection.
- Canis Rex's targeting / sensor capability is damaged.
- Matthael eventually stops trying to be the finisher and creates an opening for the Crew.
- Mira, Eryn, Barten and Norbyn each contribute to the pacification sequence.
- The Crew severs the controlling connection and brings Canis Rex down together.
- Matthael interacts directly with the remaining network connection before it disappears.
- A Crown Node responds and exposes a further pathway / trace.
- Dragon Barrow becomes the next major lead / route.
- The Crew survives and takes a short rest before continuing.

## 3. CHARACTER

### Matthael
- Highly improvisational under pressure.
- Uses humor and provocation as a coping / control mechanism.
- Protective when someone has become his responsibility.
- Will physically carry an injured person rather than abandon them.
- Takes promises seriously even when presenting them casually.
- Treats Frostfire as something embodied rather than merely an external spell effect.
- Will deliberately stop attacking when continuing would risk killing an opponent unnecessarily.
- Comfortable using himself, his surroundings and improvised magic as tools.

### Naevyra — Prologue state
- Found severely injured inside the facility.
- Physically exhausted and visibly damaged.
- Blond hair is described as blackened by dark magic.
- Initially dependent on Matthael for physical movement and escape.
- Her relationship with Matthael begins through rescue / survival rather than ordinary recruitment.
- **Important timeline boundary:** after the prologue, Naevyra is not automatically part of the current Crew. She is absent until the later Cemetery rescue.

### Eryn
- Male in the recovered play.
- Participates actively in the Canis Rex encounter.
- Uses control / roots to restrain rather than simply destroy.
- Functions as a contrast to Matthael's destructive approach.

### Barten
- Analytical / technically minded.
- Participates in the Canis Rex pacification through a node-disruption approach.
- His role is problem-solving rather than brute force.

### Mira
- Analytical and observant.
- Uses the damaged control system against Canis Rex.
- Contributes to identifying / destabilizing the spine-string connection.

### Norbyn
- Direct, physical and reliable.
- Participates in the final physical takedown of Canis Rex after the control system has been compromised.

### Crew
- Cooperation becomes increasingly important during the Canis Rex encounter.
- The encounter establishes that Matthael does not need to land the final blow for his contribution to matter.

## 4. MECHANICS

### General play style recovered
- Strict 5e-style adjudication was intended.
- Player declares an action; DM determines whether a roll is required.
- Actions, bonus actions, reactions, movement, concentration and resources should matter.
- Creative actions are allowed, but their mechanical consequences must be adjudicated rather than granted automatically.
- Encounters should be dangerous.
- Enemies should not function merely as HP pools.
- Multi-stage boss encounters are allowed.
- Crew members should actively participate.
- Player decisions should determine outcomes rather than a prewritten result.

### Matthael — recovered mechanical behavior
- Frostfire Riftblade.
- Frostfire-based improvised attacks / effects.
- Sorcerous Burst.
- Haste.
- Misty Step.
- Shield.
- Counterspell.
- Dragon’s Breath.
- Chill Touch.
- Dash.
- Produce Flame / Frostfire Spark-style environmental interaction.
- Detect Thoughts was used during the facility investigation.
- Frostfire can be used as a detection / investigation visualization method.

### Canis Rex encounter structure
- Designed as a dangerous encounter for a lower-level Crew.
- Multi-stage encounter.
- Stage II introduces transformation and a control / leash system.
- The objective can shift from damage to pacification / severing the control mechanism.
- Matthael can create battlefield control rather than simply deal damage.
- Crew members can complete different parts of a shared disable sequence.

### Frostfire Web / Mosaic
- Matthael creates an improvised Frostfire field / web / mosaic around Canis Rex.
- Intended functions include restricting movement, creating difficult terrain and giving the Crew an opportunity to work on the control mechanism.
- This is an emergent creative combat mechanic, not automatically a permanent spell definition.
