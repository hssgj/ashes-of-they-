## Q27 — Why They Joined / Stayed

### Recruitment
- Eryn, Mira, and Norbyn were recruited after Matthael arrived at the Stonehill Inn following the Icespire / manticore events.
- Barten joined later.
- Naevyra joined after the Crew rescued her.
- She had nowhere else to go, wanted revenge, and chose to go with them when given the option.

### Why they stayed
- The members simply fit together unusually well from early on.
- No one found a sufficient reason to say: "I'm leaving."
- There were repeated situations involving serious injury or willingness to sacrifice for another Crew member.
- Specific examples of these sacrifices were not exhaustively identified during Round 2.

### Current state
- Leaving alone would be practically dangerous given the current dungeon situation.
- More importantly, they do not want to let one another die.
- Each member is, in some sense, worse off without the others.

### Structural intent (NOT future canon)
- Individual characters may receive future spotlight arcs and relic-focused story material.
- Rootglass Kennel — Eryn spotlight.
- Dragonbarrow — Matthael / Ti spotlight.
- Temple of Kossuth — intended Naevyra spotlight.
- These are structural/creative intentions, not automatic future-event canon.

**STATUS: CONFIRMED / CLOSED**
