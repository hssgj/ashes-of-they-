- `RESEARCH` = explicitly unresolved question requiring later investigation.
- `FORGE` = literary/source material useful for development but not itself a continuity fact.

## Q31 — Riftblade / Crownbreaker

### ITEM — CANON
- Matthael's current sword is named **Riftblade**.
- Riftblade is Matthael's main weapon.
- Riftblade has a magical consciousness.
- Riftblade communicates through vibration, magic, and ethereal feeling rather than ordinary speech.
- Matthael naturally understands Riftblade's communication.
- Riftblade is highly compatible with Matthael.
- Riftblade functions as a conduit for Matthael's magic.
- Riftblade has the established abilities Rift Step, Scorch, and Freeze.
- Riftblade's material is silver from the bone of Matthael's dragon.
- The material and history of Riftblade were provided as source evidence and are confirmed for use in continuity.

### ITEM / FUTURE-CANON
- **Crownbreaker** is a name associated with the sword's future significance.
- The sword is currently called Riftblade.
- The exact meaning of the name Crownbreaker is deliberately reserved for the future.
- The possibility that the name Crownbreaker functions as a microscopic foreshadowing is intentionally spoiler-locked.

### ITEM / RESEARCH
- Who created Riftblade is unresolved.
- How Riftblade came to be in the dungeon location is unresolved.

### FORGE — SIDED OUT
- **How Matthael Burned His First Dungeon** is source material for Matthael's first dungeon and Riftblade awakening imagery, but does not answer the outstanding historical questions and is not itself being promoted as canon.

## Q32 — Matthael & Crew

### CREW / CANON
- The Crew is a real in-world group rather than merely a gameplay sidekick system.
- The Crew members have different specializations that complement one another.
- Matthael repeatedly chooses Crew over his own survival.
- Chaincaller recognized this pattern and weaponized the Crew's bonds.
- The Crew can accomplish things together that no individual member can accomplish alone.

### AUTHORIAL / NON-IN-WORLD
- The Crew originally arose from the practical circumstances of solo play.
- The gameplay origin is not treated as an in-world explanation.

- The individual relationships inside the Crew are not identical.

- The Crew believes they can accomplish something together that nobody can accomplish alone.

## Q34 — Why They Stay / Individual Identity

### CHARACTER — Eryn — CANON
- Eryn joined the Crew early.
- Eryn was interested in Matthael from their first meeting.
- Eryn has a strong reflex to protect and restrain Matthael.
- Rootglass Kennel is an Eryn spotlight.

### CHARACTER — Mira — CANON
- Mira respects Matthael's courage and stubbornness.
- Mira understands impossible people and systems in her own way.
- Mira has a strong relationship of mutual respect with Barten.
- Mira is not romantically paired with Barten.

### CHARACTER — Barten — CANON
- Barten is an Earth Gnome.
- Barten is an Artificer.
- Barten's specialized role is Relay Architect.
- Barten is a support engineer, battlefield analyst, device-breaker, and anti-chain technician.
- Barten's primary strength is understanding systems that ordinary people cannot describe.
- Barten is physically small and weak.
- Barten's recommended base attributes are STR 8, DEX 14, CON 14, and INT 17.
- Barten's main tool is a Relay Engine / containment-disruptor.
- Barten is not a sniper or rifleman.
- Silverstrain is not Barten's surname.
- Barten is the architect of Crew survival rather than a generic support NPC.
- Barten is analytical, anxious, nervous, physically insecure, and extremely intelligent.
- Barten has dry, literal, technical humor.
- Barten is afraid but continues anyway.
- Barten's courage is acting despite fear.
- Barten's main weakness is fear of being useless.
- Barten expresses affection through repairs, corrections, safety measures, and practical help.
- Barten is not suicidal.
- Barten is prepared, terrified, and correct.
- Barten can read impossible systems.
- Barten can analyze relays, identify architectural errors, distinguish mechanisms from projections, disrupt containment, read chain/relay logic, create anti-chain solutions, predict system failure, and turn system errors into new paths.
- Barten's Impossible Read allows him to analyze an impossible effect and give the Crew a way to respond.
- Barten identified the Pre-Wound Signature / the principle that Chaincaller leaves a wound before an attack.
- Barten is called the Missing Architect.

### CHARACTER — Barten — BOOK I CANON
- Barten prepared the Silverstrain Antichain Relay.
- The Silverstrain Antichain Relay requires a conscious anchor.
- Barten chose himself as the conscious anchor.
- Barten's relay action stabilized the Crew.
- Barten's relay action stopped the containment collapse.
- Barten's relay action interrupted Chaincaller regeneration.
- Barten's relay action interrupted Ixrathul influence.
- Barten's relay action reset Chain Pressure.
- Barten's relay action created an opportunity for the Crew's final attack.
- Barten disappeared into the Silverstrain lattice.

### CHARACTER — Barten — PLAYER TRUTH
- The player-facing truth at the end of Book I is that Barten is gone.
- Barten's body is not found.
- Barten's notebook, relay work, calculations, and traces remain.
- The epilogue contains an Empty Chair.
- Most of Barten's notebook pages are burned and one page survives.

### CHARACTER — Barten — FUTURE-CANON / DM ONLY
- Barten did not die.
- Silverstrain did not destroy Barten.
- Barten's consciousness-pattern was transferred into the Silverstrain lattice / Relay consequence space.
- Barten is not physically accessible.
- Barten remains somewhere inside the system.

### CHARACTER — Ti — CANON
- Ti is the same recurring smoke-draconic familiar / companion described in this continuity.
- Ti began as a familiar / companion relationship with the player.
- Emotionally, Matthael considers Ti his child.
- Ti was built from Matthael's essence.
- When Ti first developed consciousness, he began imprinting Matthael's traits into his personality.
- Ti's personality developed through discovery, chaos, vocabulary growth, emotional stabilization, and increasing control of his power.
- Ti's compatibility with Matthael's power developed over Book I.
- Ti became a member of the Crew before the Chaincaller fight.
- Ti is an emotional anchor more than a scout.
- Ti knows something is happening around Barten but cannot calculate or prove what is happening.
- Ti recognizes Barten's anxiety and likes Barten.
- Ti supports Barten emotionally.
- Ti is emotionally affected by Barten's disappearance in a way comparable to Matthael's reaction.
- Ti senses a Silverstrain fracture in Barten's essence and therefore does not experience Barten as simply dead.
- Ti's **"Not yours."** moment expresses his recognition that the Crew is his and he is part of the Crew.
- Ti knows what the Crew is doing at that moment and what he must protect.

### CHARACTER — Norbyn — CANON
- Norbyn is a member of the Crew.
- Norbyn joined the Crew after the victory over Cryovain / Icespire.

### CHARACTER — Naevyra — CANON
- Naevyra is the same person referred to in the chat as Naev, Naeva, and Naevera.
- Naevyra is a member of the Crew.
- Naevyra joined after the Crew rescued her.
- Naevyra had nowhere else to go at that point.
- Naevyra wanted revenge.
- Naevyra was given the option to leave and chose to stay with the Crew.
- Naevyra is a significant healer within the Crew.

- Eryn reassessed his tendency toward self-sacrifice.
- Eryn became more willing to restrain Matthael's extremes.
- Mira became sharper, crueler, and less humorous while becoming more caring toward the Crew.
- Norbyn became more serious and wiser.
- Norbyn's relationship with Grudgebell developed beyond grief into deeper resonance.
- Naevyra found her own light and became a source of light for the others.
- Ti became stronger and more stable and approached another Omen.
- Matthael became more determined to recover Barten.
- Matthael became willing to destroy threats that endanger the Crew.
- Barten's disappearance is the payoff of his analytical character arc.

### CREW / CANON
- Book I moved the Crew from merely surviving together toward actively choosing one another.
